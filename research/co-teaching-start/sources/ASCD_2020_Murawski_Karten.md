# ASCD (2020): "Co-Teaching Strategies: Dos, Don'ts, and Do Betters." W. Murawski and T. Karten
URL: https://www.ascd.org/el/articles/co-teaching-strategies-dos-donts-and-do-betters | Access: OPENED (full text) | Published: July 1, 2020 (Vol. 62, No. 7)
Type: professional journal (practitioner). The authors are co-teaching experts; Murawski also runs a consulting firm.
## Key points (Reported, expert)
- Do:
  - Get to know each other: strengths, preferences, interests, pet peeves.
  - Establish roles and responsibilities.
  - Work out differences privately.
  - Give each other physical and mental space.
- Don't:
  - Leave your partner out of planning.
  - Teach to the middle (use UDL instead).
  - Blame the schedule (co-plan creatively: shared documents, messages, video chat).
  - Worry about not knowing all the content. Special educators bring access strategies and "leave the expertise to the general educator."
- Do better:
  - High-leverage practices.
  - Co-plan at the mega (year), macro (unit or quarter), and micro (daily) levels.
  - Reflect on your responses to each other.
  - Share anecdotal notes in an agreed-upon format.
  - Keep learning.
## Quotes
- "It requires far more than putting two adults in a classroom with students"
- "Establish roles and responsibilities."
