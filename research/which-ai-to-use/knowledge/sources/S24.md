## Learned points
1. ChatGPT is still "far and away" the largest consumer AI product. It is 2.7x Gemini (#2) on web monthly traffic and 2.5x on mobile MAU, as of January 2026. [Documented (third-party data)]
2. ChatGPT reportedly grew weekly active users by 500 million in a year to 900 million, which a16z says is "over 10% of the global population" every week. [Reported]
3. Gemini and Claude paid subscriber growth sped up. Per Yipit Data (Jan 2026), Claude paid subscribers grew over 200% YoY and Gemini 258%. ChatGPT is still 8x Claude and 4x Gemini in U.S. paid subscribers. [Reported]
4. Multi-tenanting: about 20% of weekly ChatGPT web users also use Gemini in the same week. [Reported]
5. Why the competition caught up, per a16z: Google's creative models (Nano Banana generated 200M images and brought 10M new Gemini users in its first week; Veo 3 video) and Anthropic's prosumer push (Cowork, Claude in Chrome, Excel/PowerPoint plug-ins, Claude Code). [Reported]
6. Lock-in thesis: "Context compounds." Memory and connectors (calendar, email, CRM) raise switching costs. ChatGPT sessions per user per month are 1.3x Gemini's on web and 2.2x on mobile. [Documented (a16z analysis) / Inferred]
7. App ecosystems differ. As of late February 2026, ChatGPT had 220 apps in 13 categories, and Claude had about 160 curated connectors plus about 50 community MCP servers. They share only 41 apps (about 11%), mostly the horizontal productivity stack. ChatGPT has 85+ consumer transaction apps (travel, shopping, food, health), while Claude's exclusives skew professional (financial data, developer infrastructure, PubMed, Clinical Trials). [Documented (a16z count)]
8. Positioning: Anthropic "appears to be focused on the AI power users" (developers, knowledge workers). OpenAI aims at the mass market and is testing ads and planning "Sign in with ChatGPT." [Reported / Inferred by a16z]
9. Geography is splitting into three ecosystems. Western tools (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity) share top markets: the US, India, Brazil, UK, and Indonesia. DeepSeek bridges China (33.5% of web traffic), Russia (7.1%), and the US (6.6%). Russia is now a distinct third pole (Yandex Alice at 71M MAU, GigaChat). [Documented (third-party data)]
10. Per-capita AI adoption index: Singapore is first, then UAE, Hong Kong, and South Korea. The US ranks 20th. [Documented (a16z index)]
11. Creative tools: image generation is being bundled into the general assistants (GPT Image 1.5, Nano Banana). Midjourney fell from the top 10 in the first edition to #46. Video (Kling, Hailuo, Pixverse; Veo 3) and music/voice (Suno #15, ElevenLabs on every edition) hold standalone slots. [Documented]
12. Sora 2 is missing from the mobile list despite a fast launch (20 days at the top of the U.S. App Store). Sensor Tower still counts 3M+ daily mobile users. [Reported]
13. Agents: vibe coding (Replit, Lovable, Claude Code via Claude) is retentive among technical users but "has not yet cracked the true mainstream." OpenClaw (an open-source local agent) hit 68,000 GitHub stars in weeks and was acquired by OpenAI in February 2026. Manus was acquired by Meta (Dec 2025, est. $2B). Genspark reports a $100M run rate. [Reported]
14. Claude Code reached a $1B annualized revenue run rate in six months. OpenAI reported 2M weekly Codex users as of early March 2026, growing 25% weekly. [Reported]
15. Methodology caveat from a16z itself: rankings "increasingly undercount the AI products people use most." Desktop and CLI tools (Claude Code), dictation, in-app AI (Claude in Excel, Gemini in Workspace), and AI browsers (Atlas, Comet, Dia) don't show up in web or mobile traffic. [Documented]
16. The list now includes AI-first features inside existing products (CapCut with 736M MAU, Canva, Notion, Grammarly). Notion's paid AI attach rate rose from 20% to over 50% in a year. [Reported]
17. For choosing a platform: popularity (traffic) and "which is best" are different questions. The big assistants are diverging by audience (mass consumer versus professional/power user) and by ecosystem (Google Workspace versus connectors versus consumer apps). [Inferred]

## Limitations
- The ranked lists themselves (positions 1–50 for web and mobile) were images. Only the alphabetical product names were readable, so exact ranks beyond those quoted in the text are not recorded.
- Traffic and MAU measure usage, not quality, accuracy, or fitness for teaching.
- VC firm with portfolio interests. Some figures come from portfolio companies or third-party panels that a16z says it has not independently verified.
- Data are from January to early March 2026, about 8–10 months old at reading.
- The 900M WAU and subscriber-growth figures are relayed from company statements or Yipit, not primary data.
