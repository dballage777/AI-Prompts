## Learned points
1. What is measured: models are ranked by tokens processed through the OpenRouter API, counting both prompt and completion tokens, aggregated daily in UTC, per model variant (so free variants are ranked separately). Private requests are excluded. [Documented]
2. OpenRouter's own warning: the token rankings measure usage, not quality. "None of them measures users or spend." Models differ in verbosity and tokenization, and the rankings "do not rank models by accuracy, reasoning ability, or benchmark performance." [Documented]

## Limitations
- The per-task, per-domain, programming-language, open/closed share and cost-per-session data is JS-only and was not captured. The by-task view is the one that would matter most for task-specific recommendations.
- The data covers only OpenRouter traffic and is skewed toward developers, agents and price-sensitive workloads. Direct traffic to OpenAI, Anthropic and Google, and consumer app use, are not included.
- Token totals are inflated by verbose or reasoning models and by agent loops.
- The data is a snapshot that changes daily.
