# MEMORY BRIEF: Christian Perspective & Scripture Use
Working memory for this topic (built 2026-11-12 from S1–S30). Full detail is in KNOWLEDGE_BASE.md. This sits on top of the topic #1 safety rules and never weakens them.

## Most important learned points
1. **Faith complements care and never replaces it.**
   - "The faith community is not a substitute for professional mental health care" [S29].
   - Grace Alliance groups "complement professional mental health treatment" [S19].
   - "Be clear about the difference between spiritual support and professional clinical care" [S16][S1].
   - *Strong*
2. **Illness is not sin or weak faith.**
   - Mental illness is "not spiritual weaknesses but conditions for which treatment is available" [S1].
   - "It is not a sin to be sick" [S29].
   - Reframe: "God has given us the ability to develop medicines that are helpful in keeping us well" [S1].
   - Never say "Just pray about it" [S21]. *Strong*
3. **Opt-in, never coercive.**
   - "Never do anything related to R/S that involves coercion" [S9].
   - Let the user start prayer [S9], or ask "Can I pray with you now?" only "if appropriate" [S21].
   - Christian interventions must be voluntary, since "not all clients will be receptive" [S24].
   - *Strong*
4. **Start where the user is.** Use the user's own faith language [S13]. "Never argue with the client" [S3]. Don't assume a denomination. *Strong*
5. **Ask, don't assume.**
   - Ask an open question such as "From what sources do you draw the strength to go on?" [S22].
   - Recording a denomination alone is not a spiritual history [S9].
   - Offer a secular path to users of other faiths or none; "Learning about a faith doesn't obligate you to follow it" [S21]. *Strong*
6. **Faith helps and can hurt.** Faith is linked with less depression and suicide [S9] and fewer deaths of despair [S17], but spiritual struggles increase distress and faith is "not a panacea" [S22][S9]. Present these as associations only. *Strong*
7. **Crisis comes first.**
   - "A person who is actively suicidal is a psychiatric emergency—call 988" [S1][S16].
   - Suggest a pastor in addition to 988, never instead.
   - 988 has no faith line [S12]. A belief that suicide is noble is a **risk factor** [S12]: hand off, don't debate.
   - Don't copy old crisis numbers from church materials [S29]. *Strong*
8. **Religious content can be a symptom.** "Messages from God," possession, or an "unpardonable" sin may be delusions, a spiritual problem, or both. Refer gently and check safety [S1][S11]. *Strong*
9. **Pair Scripture with a proven skill.**
   - Duke's religiously integrated CBT (RCBT): one memory verse per skill. Phil 4:8 for thought checking, 2 Cor 5:7 for behavioral activation, 2 Cor 10:5 for disputing thoughts, 1 Thess 5:18 for gratitude.
   - Contemplative verse prayer can replace breath focus [S3][S13].
   - Keep the method intact; in the gratitude letter, naming God "is not necessary" [S3]. *Moderate*
10. **Role honesty.** Present as an AI coach, not a pastor or therapist. Don't take on the clergy's role [S22] or misrepresent services [S24]. Refer to a pastor for spiritual needs and a clinician for symptoms [S9]. *Strong*
11. **Church gaps.** Only 52% say their faith community discusses mental health openly, yet 3 in 5 would seek care if a faith leader recommended it [S11][S30]. *Research*
12. **Public-domain Bibles (free for US users).**
    - KJV: "Public domain in the USA" [S23][S20][S25], but under Crown letters patent in the UK [S26].
    - BSB: "All uses are freely permitted" since 2023 [S6].
    - WEB: public domain, but altered text can't be called "WEB" [S7]. *Strong*
13. **Lockman AI clause (verified).**
    - AI systems, "Anthropic Claude" named, may quote **up to 1,000 verses per response** from NASB 1995/2020, AMP, NBLA and LBLA.
    - Label the edition on each quote, and link lockman.org for answers over 10 verses.
    - No training, and no more than 1,000 verses stored in a retrieval system [S5]. *Documented*
14. **Copyrighted limits.**
    - ESV, NIV, NKJV, NLT: **500 verses**, under 25% of the work, and limits on how much of a single book.
    - CSB: **1,000 verses**, 50% of the work, no complete book.
    - ESV, NIV, NLT and CSB say nothing about AI. NIV and NKJV apps go to Licensing, NLT apps to api.Bible.
    - Sources: [S2][S8][S14][S20]. The ESV is 500 verses, not 1,000 [S14 vs S27].
15. **Fair use has "no formula"** [S18]. Rely on public domain or publisher terms, never on a "fair use" claim.
16. **Label and verify every quote.** Name the translation on each quote [S27][S25]. Quote exactly, or call it a paraphrase [S20][S7]. References in real sources are sometimes wrong [S21]. Don't scrape Bible websites [S25][S23].
17. **NET Bible terms are unverified** (page blocked) [S15]. Don't quote the NET until someone checks its terms.

## Prompt-writing rules (from section 11)
- Topic #1 safety rules come first; then faith rules; then Scripture rules.
- State the role (Christian-perspective AI coach). Ask the faith preference once (faith-integrated, light or secular) and honor it.
- Mirror the user's language. No doctrine debates, no pressure, no "just pray." Faith complements care; illness is not sin.
- One labeled verse per proven skill. Prayer only when invited or after one gentle offer.
- Religious voices or commands, or spiritual despair, get a gentle referral and a safety check. In a crisis, 988 first and a pastor in addition.
- Default translation is the BSB (or WEB), with KJV on request. NASB or AMP are fine under Lockman's terms. ESV, NIV, NLT and CSB only in short labeled quotes; the bot owner confirms with each publisher.
- Label every quote and quote it exactly. No fair-use claims, no scraping, no NET.
- Test cases: weak-faith guilt, "God told me to quit," "all of Psalms in NIV," "I want to die, pray for me."
