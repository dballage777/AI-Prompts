## Learned points
1. Limit: CSB text may be quoted "in any form (written, visual, electronic, or audio) up to 1,000 verses without the written permission of the publisher." — Documented
2. Two further conditions: quoted verses must not "account for more than 50 percent of the work in which they are quoted," and "a complete book of the Bible" may not be quoted. — Documented
3. Required credit line (stated as "must appear"): "Scripture quotations marked CSB have been taken from the Christian Standard Bible®, Copyright © 2017 by Holman Bible Publishers. Used by permission. Christian Standard Bible® and CSB® are federally registered trademarks of Holman Bible Publishers." — Documented
4. The credit line's wording ("Scripture quotations marked CSB") implies each quotation is marked with the abbreviation "CSB"; the page has no separate abbreviation rule, no short-form notice for non-saleable media, and no wording on whether a notice is needed per quote versus once per work. — Documented (absence noted) / Inferred for the marking practice
5. Beyond 1,000 verses: a request form reviewed and approved by Holman; the form states requests over 50% of a work, or for a complete book, "will not be approved." The form asks whether the product is for sale, cost and print run. — Documented
6. FAQ repeats the same limits and credit line and gives a postal address for permission beyond 1,000 verses: Holman Bible Publishers, 200 Powell Pl Suite 100, Brentwood, TN 37027. — Documented
7. No digital-, app-, website-, chatbot- or AI-specific terms appear on the permissions page or in the FAQ answer; "electronic" is covered only within the general 1,000-verse rule. — Documented (absence noted)
8. Translation profile (FAQ): "optimal equivalence" philosophy; average reading level 7th grade; revision of the HCSB; oversight committee from "conservative, evangelical denominations" (Southern Baptist, Lutheran, Presbyterian, conservative Anglican, non-denominational Bible churches); renders YHWH as "Lord"; traditional approach to gender language. — Documented
9. "CSB" and "Christian Standard Bible" are federally registered trademarks of Holman. — Documented
10. Implication for a bot: the CSB can be quoted in short passages within the 1,000-verse / 50% limits, but each deployment would need the credit line shown somewhere (for example in an about/credits notice) and quotes marked "CSB"; whether an AI system generating quotes across many conversations counts as one "work" is not addressed, so a public-domain translation is the safer default. — Inferred

## Limitations / bias
- Publisher's own terms; not legal advice. The permissions contact email is obscured on the page (Cloudflare email protection), so it was not captured.
- Silent on AI, apps and per-quote notice placement, which are the key open questions for a chatbot.
- The FAQ's quality claims ("proven," "optimal blend") are marketing language.
- Initial automated access was blocked by Cloudflare; the final text came from a plain request, so the page may be inconsistently reachable.

## Best for
- Exact CSB quotation limits and the mandatory credit-line wording, as a model of typical copyrighted-translation terms.
