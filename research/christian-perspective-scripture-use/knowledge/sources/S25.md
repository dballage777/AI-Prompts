## Learned points
1. Site-wide quotation allowance: material "may be quoted in any form (written, visual, electronic or audio), up to and inclusive of two hundred fifty (250) verses or five hundred (500) words of non-biblical text" without written permission. — [Documented]
2. Two conditions on that allowance: the quoted verses must not "amount to a complete book of the Bible," and must not account for "twenty-five (25) percent or more of the total text of the work in which they are quoted." — [Documented]
3. Notice rule: for quotes within the allowance, "notice of copyright must appear on the title or copyright page or opening screen of the work." For a bot/app, the "opening screen" is the natural place. — [Documented]
4. Non-commercial exception: for "church bulletins, research projects, or similar uses," no full notice is needed, but "at minimum the initials for the translation quoted (e.g., NIV®) must appear at the end of each quotation." — [Documented]
5. Each translation can override the site rule: "Certain material on this Site may have different copyright restrictions. Please consult the copyright notices for individual Bible versions." — [Documented]
6. Example of a stricter-but-larger publisher rule (NIV page): NIV may be quoted up to "five hundred (500) verses" (same no-complete-book and under-25% conditions), with this exact notice: "THE HOLY BIBLE, NEW INTERNATIONAL VERSION®, NIV® Copyright © 1973, 1978, 1984, 2011 by Biblica, Inc.® Used by permission. All rights reserved worldwide." Non-salable media need only "NIV" after each quote. — [Documented]
7. NIV page also says the online text is "for your personal use only," and that "copying or reposting on the Internet is prohibited"; commercial commentary/reference works must get written permission; US/Canada requests go to HarperCollins Christian Publishing Permissions (Nashville); UK/EEC/EFTA to Hodder & Stoughton. "NIV" is a registered trademark of Biblica. — [Documented]
8. KJV page: "Publisher: Public Domain" and "The KJV is public domain in the United States"; Bible Gateway's KJV matches the 1987 printing; "No copyright information available." — [Documented]
9. General license: Company Content is licensed only for "personal, non-commercial use"; except as allowed, users "may not copy, download, stream capture, reproduce, duplicate, archive, upload, modify, translate, publish ... or otherwise use any Content." Services may not be used for commercial purposes without authorization. — [Documented]
10. Automated access: users may not "Use technology or any automated system such as scripts or bots in order to collect ... data from the Company Services," nor create an undue burden on servers. No clause in the retrieved Terms mentions AI, machine learning, model training, or scraping by name. — [Documented]
11. robots.txt disallows most /bible/ passage paths for all user-agents and sets "Crawl-delay: 15"; no AI-specific crawler rules seen. Together with point 10, a bot should not fetch or scrape Bible Gateway at runtime. — [Documented]
12. Widget Terms (by "BibleGateway, a division of The Zondervan Corporation"): license to use widgets "solely for your personal use"; no incorporating widgets into "any hardware or software device," no distribution, sublicensing or deriving income without written permission. Rules out embedding Bible Gateway widgets in a commercial bot. — [Documented]
13. Users may not remove or bypass copyright notices or DRM/access controls (including geo-filtering). Governed by New York law; jury-trial and class-action waivers. — [Documented]
14. Practical bot rule (synthesis): source verse text from a public-domain translation held locally (e.g., KJV, see S23) or obtain a publisher license; if quoting a copyrighted translation under its fair-use clause, keep quotes well below the verse caps, show the publisher's notice on the opening screen, and tag each quote with the translation initials. — [Inferred]

## Limitations / bias
- Commercial publisher's terms, written to protect its licensed content; the site-wide 250-verse rule and per-version rules (NIV 500) differ, and the Terms say version notices control.
- Terms are silent on AI/LLM use and do not define "commercial" for a free bot; legal questions should go to the publisher's permissions office.
- Only NIV and KJV version pages were checked; ESV, NLT, CSB, NKJV and others have their own notices not captured here.
## Best for
- Exact Bible Gateway quotation caps, notice placement and per-quote initials, plus the no-scraping and personal-use limits that rule out live fetching in a bot.
