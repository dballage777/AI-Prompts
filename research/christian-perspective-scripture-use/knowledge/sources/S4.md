## Learned points
1. ESV gratis limit: the text may be quoted "in written, visual, or electronic form" up to and including **500 consecutive verses** without written permission. Two conditions apply: the verses must not amount to **more than one-half of any one book** of the Bible (or its equivalent measured in bytes), and they must not account for **25% or more of the total text** of the work. — [Documented]
2. Audio use (cassettes, CDs, audio television) follows the same rule: up to 500 verses, at most half of a book, and under 25% of the work. — [Documented]
3. Required full notice (title or copyright page, "or in a corresponding location when the ESV is quoted in other media"): "Scripture quotations are from The ESV® Bible (The Holy Bible, English Standard Version®), © 2001 by Crossway, a publishing ministry of Good News Publishers. Used by permission. All rights reserved." — [Documented]
4. Multiple translations: the notice should begin "Unless otherwise indicated, all Scripture quotations are from…" or "Scripture quotations marked (ESV) are from…". — [Documented]
5. Abbreviation rule: in **non-saleable** print and digital media (church bulletins, orders of service, posters, transparencies, or similar), a complete notice is not required, but "the initials (ESV) must appear at the end of the quotation." — [Documented]
6. Commercial commentary or Bible reference works that use the ESV must have written permission, whatever the verse count. — [Documented]
7. "ESV," "English Standard Version," "Global Study Bible," and the ESV logo are registered trademarks, and using them requires Crossway's prior permission. This is relevant if a product brands itself with "ESV." — [Documented]
8. Requests over the limits go to Crossway, Attn: Bible Rights, Wheaton, IL. Anglicized ESV requests go to HarperCollins Religious (London). — [Documented]
9. The ESV is adapted from the Revised Standard Version (copyright National Council of the Churches of Christ in the U.S.A.). — [Documented]
10. All study notes, maps, charts, introductions, and cross-references in the Global Study Bible are separately copyrighted (2008–2012). The gratis rule covers the ESV *text*, not study material. — [Documented]
11. The page contains **no app-, website-, or AI-specific terms**. The only digital terms are "electronic form" in the gratis grant, "other media" for notice placement, and "non-saleable … digital media" for the abbreviation shortcut. It notes the ESV is free to read online and on mobile at esv.org. — [Documented (absence noted)]
12. For a bot: whether a chatbot is "non-saleable digital media" (abbreviation-only allowed) or needs the full notice "in a corresponding location" is not addressed. A cautious design labels each quote "(ESV)" and places the full notice in the app's about or credits screen. — [Inferred]
13. This page is the copyright page of one specific product (the ESV Global Study Bible). Crossway's general permissions page may differ and was not read here. — [Inferred]
