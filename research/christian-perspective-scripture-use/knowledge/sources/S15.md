2. The actual terms appear to live at bible.org/copyright (anchor #cpyrt), but that page returned 403 Forbidden to the standard fetch tool, so its content is NOT recorded here. — Documented
3. The About page names no publisher for the NET Bible text and gives no licensing or free-use terms. It only says the tool is "a resource by Bible.org," with footer "© 2014 Bible.org," and links an iOS app. — Documented (summary-only)
4. The reader offers several translations side by side (NET2, ESV, HCSB, ISV, KJV, MSG, NASB) and has a "NET Notes" toggle. That shows the site hosts other publishers' texts too, so the site's terms may not cover every version displayed. — Documented / Inferred
5. NOT VERIFIED: any NET verse-count limit, percentage limit, required notice wording, free-use or "ministry use" terms, notes-usage rules, or app/AI terms. None of these may be stated in the bot prompt on the strength of this source. — Documented gap
6. Bot implications: until the NET terms are verified from an accessible official page, the bot prompt should not claim the NET Bible is "free to quote" or give NET notice wording. Use a translation whose terms were verified (for example, the ESV limits in S14) or a public-domain translation from another verified source. — Inferred
