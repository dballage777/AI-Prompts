## Learned points
1. [Research] Purpose: to improve understanding of which information features are relevant to users seeking anime recommendations.
2. [Research] Method: content analysis of 396 recommendation-request threads from the Anime News Network online forum.
3. [Research] Finding: 19 important anime information features were identified, including Work, Theme, Genre, Audience, and Mood.
4. [Research] Artwork/Visual Style, Audio Style, and Language were mentioned less often. But when users did mention them, they discussed them "with specificity and depth."
5. [Research] Limitations stated by the authors: a relatively small sample of 396 records, and no demographic information about the users.
6. [Research] Practical implication stated: the findings can be built into anime databases and information systems to improve organization, browsing and retrieval, and recommendation. The authors say this could extend to other audiovisual materials.
7. [Research] The authors position it as one of the few studies of what anime users need and want.
8. [Research] The record shows 11 Scopus citations and keywords: animation, anime, content analysis, information needs, multimedia, query analysis, recommendation.
9. [Inferred] "Work" appearing among the top features fits the common pattern of asking for something like a named title. Style features being less frequent but highly specific suggests a recommender should ask about them explicitly rather than assume they don't matter.

## Limitations or bias
- Abstract only. The full list of 19 features, their definitions, frequencies, and ranks was not seen.
- Forum posters are a self-selected, engaged fan population, and the data has no demographics.
- The record notes the work was published while the authors were at the University of Washington (externally published).
