## Learned points
1. [Reported] Usage context: the post cites a Pew Research Center survey that 83% of U.S. adults use streaming services, and a Nielsen report that streaming has overtaken broadcast and cable combined. Its point is that screen fandom is a large entry point for advisory. (Figures are reported second-hand; the underlying reports were not opened.)
2. [Documented] NoveList offers "For Fans Of" curated lists of books that appeal to fans of trending movies, TV shows, and other pop culture. The lists are available at all reading levels and described as "ever-evolving," and the post calls them one of NoveList's most popular resources.
3. [Documented] Related NoveList features: the "Fandemonium!" theme and a bimonthly "Page to Screen" NextReads newsletter. LibraryAware has customizable "For Fans Of" display designs searchable by keyword.
4. [Documented] Example translations in the post: Heated Rivalry (hockey romance) leads to "steamy sports romances," and The Pitt (real-time medical drama) leads to "real-time stories with ensemble casts." These translate a show into a mix of genre or subject (sports romance) and structural or appeal features (steamy tone, real-time pacing, ensemble cast). Other lists mentioned: Bridgerton and Stranger Things.
5. [Documented] Stated use case: the gap after a binge ends while "the next season is still a year (or more!) away." Advisory fills the wait between seasons.
6. [Documented] Users can suggest new For Fans Of lists via an in-product "Share Feedback" link or by email. The lists are editorially curated, not algorithmic.

- The statistics are second-hand; the claim of popularity is unquantified.
