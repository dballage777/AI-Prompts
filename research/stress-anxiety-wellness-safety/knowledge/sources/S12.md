## Learned points
1. JED's stance: it does not oppose AI innovation but wants youth safety built into AI design and policy from the start. — [Documented, summary-only]
2. Risks named: distorted reality and eroded trust (misinformation, body-image harm, deepfakes); manipulation young people cannot detect, such as algorithmic nudging; chatbots or AI search summaries that surface harmful content or fail to alert human support when a young person is in distress. — [Documented, summary-only]
3. Simulated companionship can foster dependency, delay help-seeking, and displace real friendships. — [Documented, summary-only]
4. Bias risk: AI may misread or exclude LGBTQIA+ youth, youth of color, and youth with disabilities. — [Documented, summary-only]
5. Core crisis principle: AI tools should "always prioritize connection to trusted, human support," especially for people in distress. — [Documented, summary-only]
6. Six recommendations for companies: design around development and emotional well-being, and test emotional safety before deployment; ban emotionally manipulative and dependency-forming features; provide transparency and human oversight (users should know they are talking to AI and what data is collected); limit inference of sensitive emotional data and audit for bias and harm; involve young people in design and governance; invest in AI literacy. — [Documented, summary-only]
7. JED says AI companions should be banned outright for minors except under strict clinical supervision. — [Documented, summary-only]
8. Nine policy actions, including: age-appropriate design standards (limits on deceptive design, autoplay, and addictive mechanics); privacy-preserving age verification; impact assessments and public disclosure of safety studies; a ban on behavioral targeting of minors; protection of youth data and likenesses, including deepfakes; research to back claimed benefits; FTC rulemaking and private rights of action; and a National Center for Youth and AI Ethics. — [Documented, summary-only]
9. Crisis resources on the page: call, text, or chat the 988 Lifeline, or text HOME (or HOLA for Spanish) to 741-741 for Crisis Text Line. The site says it does not give medical advice. — [Documented, summary-only]
10. JED states that age alone is not a reliable marker of readiness for these tools. — [Documented, summary-only]
11. For an adult-focused bot: the transferable rules are to disclose that it is AI, avoid dependency-building or companion-style manipulation, steer distressed users toward real people (friends, family, professionals, 988), and avoid inferring sensitive emotional data beyond what the user shares. — [Inferred]
