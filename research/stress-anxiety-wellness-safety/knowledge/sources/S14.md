## Learned points
1. Definitions: suicide is when people harm themselves with intent to end their life and die as a result. A suicide attempt involves the same intent without death. Suicidal ideation is "thinking about, considering, or planning suicide." Suicidal behavior is complex, and there is no single cause; people of all backgrounds can be at risk. — [Documented]
2. Warning signs (talking about): wanting to die; great guilt or shame; being a burden to others. — [Documented]
3. Warning signs (feeling): empty, hopeless, trapped, or having no reason to live; extremely sad, more anxious, agitated, or full of rage; unbearable emotional or physical pain. — [Documented]
4. Warning signs (changing behavior): making a plan or researching ways to die; withdrawing, saying goodbye, giving away important items, or making a will; taking dangerous risks such as driving extremely fast; extreme mood swings; eating or sleeping more or less; using drugs or alcohol more often. — [Documented]
5. Urgency rule: get help as soon as possible if the warning signs apply, "particularly if the behavior is new or has increased recently." — [Documented]
6. 5 Action Steps (#BeThe1To), step 1, ASK: ask directly, "Are you thinking about suicide?" NIMH says studies show asking "does not increase suicidal behavior or thoughts." — [Documented; Research cited]
7. Step 2, BE THERE: listen without judgment. Research suggests acknowledging and talking about suicide may reduce suicidal thoughts. — [Documented; Research cited]
8. Step 3, HELP KEEP THEM SAFE: ask whether they have a plan, and reduce access to highly lethal items or places (make lethal means less available or less deadly). — [Documented]
9. Step 4, HELP THEM CONNECT: connect them with the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline (call or text 988) and community resources. Help them reach a trusted family member, friend, spiritual advisor, or mental health professional. — [Documented]
10. Step 5, FOLLOW UP: stay in touch after a crisis or discharge. Studies show supportive, ongoing contact can play an important role in prevention. — [Documented; Research cited]
11. Crisis line wording: "call or text the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline at 988 or chat at 988lifeline.org. In life-threatening situations, call 911." The stats page adds that 988 is available 24/7, gives confidential support to anyone in suicidal crisis or emotional distress, offers live chat, and provides Spanish help ("Para ayuda en español, llame al 988"). — [Documented]
12. 2023 data (CDC WISQARS, via NIMH): suicide was the 11th leading cause of US deaths (49,316), the 2nd leading cause for ages 10–34, and the 4th for ages 35–44. There were more than twice as many suicides as homicides (22,830). — [Documented]
13. Rate trend: the age-adjusted rate rose 35.2%, from 10.4 per 100,000 in 2000 to 14.2 in 2018, then fell to 13.5 in 2020. It was 14.2 in 2022 and 14.1 in 2023. — [Documented]
14. Adults 2024: 5.5% (14.3 million) had serious thoughts of suicide, 4.6 million made plans, and 2.2 million (0.8%) attempted in the past year. — [Documented]
15. Other resources NIMH lists: the 2024 National Strategy for Suicide Prevention (HHS); the Ask Suicide-Screening Questions (ASQ) Toolkit for medical settings; SAMHSA's service locator; CDC, IHS, and the Suicide Prevention Resource Center. — [Documented]
16. For a bot: the warning-sign list can work as trigger criteria. The 5 steps map to bot behavior: ask directly and calmly, listen without judgment, ask about a plan and access to means, connect to 988 or 911 and trusted people (including a spiritual advisor, which fits a faith-based bot), and invite a follow-up check-in. — [Inferred]

## Limitations / bias
- The 5 Action Steps are written for a human helper (a friend or family member), not for an AI. Applying them to a chatbot is an inference.
- The "studies show" claims link to research that was not opened here.
- The topic hub mostly links out to other resources. The substance is in the subpages read.

## Best for
- An authoritative US warning-sign checklist and a 5-step crisis response script (ask, be there, keep safe, connect to 988 or 911, follow up) to adapt into the bot's crisis protocol.
