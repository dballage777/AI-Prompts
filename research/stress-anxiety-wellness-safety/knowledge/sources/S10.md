## Learned points
1. Title on the form: "Patient Safety Plan Template" — a one-page fill-in form with six numbered steps plus a closing reasons-for-living line. — Documented
2. Step 1 (exact): "Warning signs (thoughts, images, mood, situation, behavior) that a crisis may be developing:" — three blank lines. — Documented
3. Step 2 (exact): "Internal coping strategies – Things I can do to take my mind off my problems without contacting another person (relaxation technique, physical activity):" — three blank lines. — Documented
4. Step 3 (exact): "People and social settings that provide distraction:" — 1. Name / Phone; 2. Name / Phone; 3. Place; 4. Place. — Documented
5. Step 4 (exact): "People whom I can ask for help:" — three Name / Phone lines. — Documented
6. Step 5 (exact): "Professionals or agencies I can contact during a crisis:" — 1. Clinician Name / Phone / Clinician Pager or Emergency Contact #; 2. Clinician Name / Phone / Clinician Pager or Emergency Contact #; 3. Local Urgent Care Services / Urgent Care Services Address / Urgent Care Services Phone; 4. "Suicide Prevention Lifeline Phone: 1-800-273-TALK (8255)". — Documented
7. Step 6 (exact): "Making the environment safe:" — two blank lines (no examples given on the form). — Documented
8. Closing line (exact): "The one thing that is most important to me and worth living for is:" — one blank line. — Documented
9. Structure is a graded sequence: self-recognition (warning signs) → self-managed coping → social distraction → asking specific people for help → professional/crisis contacts → reducing access to means in the environment; the form itself does not explain the ordering or rationale. — Inferred
10. Copyright/use restriction printed on the form: the template is ©2008 Barbara Stanley and Gregory K. Brown, reprinted with the authors' express permission, and no portion may be reproduced without their express, written permission (author contact emails are printed on the form). — Documented
11. Outdated number: the form lists 1-800-273-TALK (8255); per S8 (SAMHSA FAQ) that number was relaunched as 988 in July 2022 and still works, so current guidance would point to 988 (call/text/chat). — Documented (cross-source)
12. Labeled a "Patient" template with clinician fields, implying it is designed to be completed with a clinician rather than by an AI or alone. — Inferred
13. Implication for a bot: a bot may mention that safety plans exist and describe the six step headings at a high level, encourage the user to make one with a counselor (988 or a clinician), but should not reproduce the template verbatim (copyright) or act as the clinician completing it; it should add 988 as the current crisis line. — Inferred

## Limitations / bias
- A blank form only: no instructions, rationale, evidence or examples (e.g., what "making the environment safe" means in practice).
- Crisis number is the pre-2022 10-digit Lifeline number.
- Copyrighted with an explicit no-reproduction clause, which constrains embedding it in a prompt or bot output.
- Intended for clinician-guided use with patients.

## Best for
- The exact six-step structure of the standard suicide safety plan, so the bot can describe it accurately and refer users to complete one with 988 or a clinician.
