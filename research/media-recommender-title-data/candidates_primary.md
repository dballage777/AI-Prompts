### Notes for the recommender (from the pages above, not from memory)
- Key-free verification paths that worked in this sandbox: AniList GraphQL, TVmaze REST, Kitsu JSON:API, Wikidata SPARQL and EntityData, and IMDb dataset TSV downloads. TMDB, MAL, IGDB, OMDb, Trakt, and Simkl all rejected keyless calls (401/403/412).
- Anime demographics: AniList returns them as tags with `category: "Demographic"` (e.g. Shounen, rank 71 for Frieren).
- Adaptation status and watch order: AniList `source` (e.g. MANGA) and `relations` edges with MediaRelation values (ADAPTATION, PREQUEL, SEQUEL, SIDE_STORY, PARENT).
- US ratings: TMDB `/movie/{id}/release_dates` (MPA certification) and `/tv/{id}/content_ratings` (TV Parental Guidelines, e.g. TV-MA). IMDb Parents Guide severity is only in the paid bulk product.
- Cross-walk: TMDB `/find` takes imdb_id/tvdb_id/wikidata_id; Wikidata P345/P4947/P4983/P8729/P4086/P5646. Wikidata models anime per season, so IDs can sit on different items.
