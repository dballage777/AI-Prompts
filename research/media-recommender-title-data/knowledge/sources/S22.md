## Learned points
1. [Documented] Purpose: store "the mapping between various anime sources". The README lists MyAnimeList, TheTVDB, TheMovieDB, Anime-Planet, Kitsu, aniSearch, AniList, AniDB, LiveChart and Anime News Network. The data also has animecountdown_id, simkl_id and imdb_id.
2. [Documented] How it is generated: the anime-lists-generator merges (1) anime-offline-database, now linked as `github.com/cedya77/anime-offline-database`, not manami-project, and (2) `Anime-Lists/anime-lists` (the AniDB→TVDB/TMDB XML mapping). The merge key is `anidb_id`, "the most common denominator of both lists". Missing TMDB, TVDB or IMDb IDs are then looked up through TheMovieDB API search or its external-ID endpoints.
3. [Documented] Files: `anime-offline-database-reduced` (IDs only), `anime-lists-reduced`, `anime-list-full` (merged on anidb_id), `anime-list-mini` (the same, minified), `Collections/` (per-source franchise groupings), and `Indices/` (per-source ID → array position lookups for both files).
4. [Documented] Record fields: `type`, `anidb_id`, `anilist_id`, `animecountdown_id`, `animenewsnetwork_id`, `anime-planet_id` (slug string), `anisearch_id`, `imdb_id` (array), `kitsu_id`, `livechart_id`, `mal_id`, `simkl_id`, `themoviedb_id` {tv: int | movie: int[]}, `tvdb_id`, `season` {tvdb, tmdb}, and `episode_offset` {tvdb, tmdb}.
5. [Documented] `themoviedb_id.tv` is always a single ID, while `themoviedb_id.movie` is an array because one AniDB entry can map to several TMDB movies. TMDB and TVDB number movies and TV separately: TMDB ID 25 is a TV show (Star Wars Droids) at /tv/25 and a different film (Jarhead) at /movie/25. Always pair the ID with the right endpoint.
6. [Documented] `season` and `episode_offset` place an anime entry, which is usually one cour or season, inside a single TVDB or TMDB series. Example: the .hack//G.U. Trilogy movie maps to TVDB 79099 at season 0 (specials) with episode_offset 11.
7. [Documented, live data check] Frieren season 1 (mal 52991 / anilist 154587) maps to TMDB tv 209867, TVDB 424536, season {tvdb 1, tmdb 1}. Frieren season 2 (mal 59978 / anilist 182255) maps to the same TMDB and TVDB series with season {tvdb 2, tmdb 1} and episode_offset {tmdb 28}. So TMDB lists season 2 as episodes 29+ of its season 1, while TVDB has a separate season 2. The two Western databases split seasons differently.
8. [Documented, live data check] Several separate anime entries share one IMDb or TVDB series. For example, 3x3 Eyes OVA (anidb 2) and Seima Densetsu (anidb 3) both have imdb tt0102847 and tvdb 70973, as seasons 1 and 2. Crest of the Stars and Banner of the Stars share tvdb 72025 and imdb tt0286390. Mapping from IMDb/TVDB/TMDB back to anime is one-to-many.
9. [Documented, live data check] anime-list-mini.json holds 39,745 records. Field coverage: type 38,418; mal_id 30,957; anime-planet 26,902; kitsu 22,411; anisearch 21,410; anilist 20,924; anidb 16,075; simkl and animecountdown 14,719; ANN 12,750; livechart 12,448; themoviedb_id 8,532; imdb_id 8,267; tvdb_id 7,518; season 7,453; episode_offset 688. Only about 21% of entries have a TMDB or TVDB link.
10. [Documented] Collections come from offline-database `relatedAnime`. They keep only bidirectional relations, "to prevent Anime that has a relation to another Anime (possibly through a crossover episode) that would produce a huge Collection". The example is AniDB "Seikai no Monshou" with IDs [1, 4, 5, 6, 884, 1623, 2673].
11. [Documented] Corrections: "this project cannot provide a way for corrections anymore". Fixes have to go to the upstream projects, and missing IDs should be added to TheMovieDB.
12. [Inferred] Recommender use: this is the bridge between anime IDs (AniList, MAL) and general TV and movie databases (TMDB, IMDb, TVDB). For example, it lets you get a TMDB ID for streaming-availability or general-catalogue lookups and then apply the season and episode offset. Download the mini file once for offline, key-free use (the raw GitHub URL worked).
13. [Inferred] Since about 79% of entries have no TMDB/TVDB/IMDb ID and those IDs are partly found by TMDB search, treat a mapped TMDB ID as likely, not certain. Check that the title and year match before relying on it.

## Limitations or bias
- The README states no licence and no LICENSE file was found at the root, so reuse terms are unclear. The upstream manami data is ODbL (see S15), which probably carries over to derived data, but that is an inference.
- Neither the update cadence nor the last-generation date is stated.
- The upstream has changed to a cedya77 fork of anime-offline-database. Its provenance and maintenance were not checked.
- The README links Kitsu at kitsu.io, while manami uses kitsu.app. The URL templates may be out of date.

## Best used for
- Cross-walking anime IDs (AniList/MAL/AniDB/Kitsu) to TMDB, TVDB and IMDb, including season and episode offsets for split-cour or multi-season shows, and seeding franchise groupings from Collections.
