## Learned points
1. [Documented] Seven key-free gzipped TSV files (UTF-8, header row, `\N` = null) are downloadable from https://datasets.imdbws.com/: `name.basics`, `title.akas`, `title.basics`, `title.crew`, `title.episode`, `title.principals`, `title.ratings` (`.tsv.gz`). No account or key is needed to download.
2. [Documented] Refresh cadence: "The data is refreshed daily." Confirmed live: the file's `x-amz-meta-run-date` was 2026-11-14 and Last-Modified 12:49 GMT the same day.
3. [Documented] `title.basics` fields: `tconst`, `titleType` (e.g. movie, short, tvseries, tvepisode, video), `primaryTitle`, `originalTitle`, `isAdult` (0/1), `startYear` (series start year for TV), `endYear` (TV series only, else `\N`), `runtimeMinutes`, `genres` (up to three). This is the core file for checking a title exists, its year, and its medium.
4. [Documented] `title.akas`: `titleId`, `ordering`, `title`, `region`, `language`, `types` (enumerated: "alternative", "dvd", "festival", "tv", "video", "working", "original", "imdbDisplay"; "New values may be added in the future without warning"), `attributes` (not enumerated), `isOriginalTitle`. Use it to match localized/English/romanized titles to one tconst.
5. [Documented] `title.episode`: `tconst`, `parentTconst`, `seasonNumber`, `episodeNumber`. Gives season/episode structure for series by IMDb numbering.
6. [Documented] `title.ratings`: `averageRating` ("weighted average of all the individual user ratings") and `numVotes`. The weighting method is not documented.
7. [Documented] No content-rating (certification), plot, keyword, franchise, or streaming-availability fields are in the free datasets. The paid bulk-data docs list sections such as "Parents Guide", "Meters", and "Box Office" that are not in the free files.
8. [Documented] Licence conditions (Help page): personal and non-commercial use only; the data "must be taken only from the datasets made available"; no "data mining, robots, screen scraping, or similar online data gathering" on the website; it may not be "altered/republished/resold/repurposed to create any kind of online/offline database of movie information (except for individual personal use)"; IMDb "reserves the right to withdraw permission to use the data at any time".
9. [Documented] Required attribution statement: "Information courtesy of IMDb (https://www.imdb.com). Used with permission."
10. [Documented] Paid IMDb API: available only through AWS Data Exchange; needs an AWS account and AWS access keys; GraphQL-backed. Products are "IMDb Essential Metadata for Movies/TV/OTT (API)" and "IMDb and Box Office Mojo for Movies/TV/OTT (API)". IMDb staff process subscription requests "within five business days" (trial available). The API key arrives by email. Endpoint: `api-fulfill.dataexchange.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/v1`.
11. [Inferred] For a personal recommender, the safe IMDb path is to download the daily TSVs locally and look up tconsts. Do not fetch imdb.com pages from a Claude Code session, because scraping the website is explicitly prohibited. Anything not in the datasets (certifications, plots) has to come from another source.
12. [Inferred] Files are large (title.basics.tsv.gz content-length was 230,369,459 bytes ≈ 230 MB compressed), so stream them (e.g. `zcat | awk`) or load them into SQLite/DuckDB rather than reading them whole into context.
13. [Inferred] Anime is usually listed as `tvSeries` with one tconst for the whole run. This granularity differs from AniList/MAL, which give each season or cour its own entry (see S10 test).

## Limitations or bias
- Ratings are IMDb user votes (weighting not disclosed). They skew toward Western, English-language audiences and IMDb's user base.
- Genres are capped at three per title, which is coarse for recommendation.
- The licence is permissive only for personal use, and permission can be withdrawn at any time. A shared or public tool would need the commercial licence.
- The "copyright/license information enclosed in each file" mentioned on the Help page was not inspected (the files were not downloaded).

## Summary-only items
- None (the paid "Bulk Data" sections such as Parents Guide and Meters were seen only as navigation labels; their contents were not read).

## Best used for
- Offline existence, year, and medium checks (`titleType`, `startYear`/`endYear`) keyed by tconst, the most widely cross-referenced ID.
- Alternate-title matching (`title.akas`) and episode structure (`title.episode`).
- A popularity/quality signal (`averageRating`, `numVotes`), with attribution.
