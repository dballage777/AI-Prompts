## Learned points
1. [Documented] "This API powers our website. Most API functions are public and do not require an account." Server: `https://api.mangaupdates.com/v1`. User registration through the API is disabled, and user features use bearer auth.
2. [Documented] Acceptable Use Policy: "You will credit MangaUpdates when using data provided by this API". Space requests reasonably and "employ caching mechanisms". Do not deceive or defraud users, act illegally, spam, or "Damage the database". No numeric rate limit is published, and the live responses carried no rate-limit headers.
3. [Documented] Warranties: "MangaUpdates makes no warranties about service availability, correctness of the data, or anything else." The service "may change at any time".
4. [Documented] Search is POST `/v1/series/search` with a JSON body (`SeriesSearchRequestV1`). Fields include `search`, `stype` (title|description), `licensed` (yes|no), `type[]`, `year`, `genre[]`, `exclude_genre[]`, `category[]`, `filter`/`filters[]` (scanlated, completed, oneshots, no_oneshots, some_releases, no_releases), `orderby` (score, title, rank, rating, year, …), `page`, and `perpage`. Results come back as `{total_hits, results[{record, hit_title, …}]}`.
5. [Documented] A series record is GET `/v1/series/{id}` (`SeriesModelV1`). Fields: `series_id`, `title`, `url`, `associated[{title}]` (alternative titles), `description`, `type` (enum: Manga, Manhwa, Manhua, Novel, Doujinshi, OEL, Artbook, Drama CD, plus country types such as Thai, French, …), `year` (string), `bayesian_rating`, `rating_votes`, `genres`, `categories` (user-voted tags), `latest_chapter` (int), `status` (free-text string), `licensed` (bool), `completed` (bool), `anime{start,end}` (strings), `related_series[]`, `authors`, `publishers[{publisher_name, type: Original|English, notes}]`, `publications[]`, `recommendations`, `rank`, `last_updated`.
6. [Documented] `related_series.relation_type` values: Prequel, Sequel, Spin-Off, Adapted From, Alternate Version, Part of Anthology, Main Story, Side Story, Full Anthology, Other.
7. [Documented, live test] Search command: `curl -s -X POST https://api.mangaupdates.com/v1/series/search -H 'Content-Type: application/json' -d '{"search":"Frieren","perpage":5}'` returned HTTP 200 with total_hits 41. The top hit was a Doujinshi ("Sousou no Frieren dj - Frieren Has a Very Bad Night"), not the main manga, which came third (id 66296374554, Manga, 2020). "Spy x Family" returned total_hits 9377, with the main series first (id 67814124606, Manga, 2019) and then doujinshi. To find the real series, filter `type == "Manga"` (or exclude Doujinshi) and check the year.

10. [Inferred, from tests 8–9] `anime.start`/`anime.end` answer "where does the anime leave off, and where do I start reading?". For example, Frieren's anime S2 ends at Vol 9, Ch 80, so a reader would continue from Ch 81. These fields are free text written by editors, with per-season segments split by " / " and notes such as "Skips …". They need careful parsing, or should be shown to the user word for word.
11. [Documented, live test] Demographics appear as genres here ("Shounen" in both records). The `categories` are many user-voted tags with vote counts (e.g. "Adapted to Anime" 9 or 17 votes, "Adapted to Movie" 8), which give weak signals for adaptation and themes.
12. [Inferred] `status` is a combined string of the original-language volume count plus a state in parentheses (Ongoing, Hiatus, and presumably Complete and others). Use `completed` (bool) for machine logic and `status` for display. `latest_chapter` tracks releases (scanlation and official) and may not equal official chapters.
13. [Inferred] `licensed` together with `publishers[type=English]` tells you whether an official English release exists and who publishes it, which is useful for recommending legal reading options. `notes` such as "Simulpub" mark simultaneous publication.

## Limitations or bias
- Data comes from the community and site editors. The anime-coverage fields are free text with no fixed schema, and some records will lack them.
- Search ranking can put doujinshi above the main series, and adult doujinshi appear in plain searches (Spy x Family results included explicit titles). Filter by `type` and genres.
- MangaUpdates has no cross-IDs to AniList or MAL in the fields reviewed. Match on title and year.
- Only two series were tested, and the "status" vocabulary beyond Ongoing and Hiatus was not checked.

## Best used for
- Key-free checks on manga adaptation status: whether a series is ongoing, on hiatus or complete, its latest chapter, where each anime season starts and ends in the manga, whether it is licensed in English and by which publisher, and the serialising magazine (a demographic signal).
