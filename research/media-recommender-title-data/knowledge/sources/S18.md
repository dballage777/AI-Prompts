## Learned points
1. [Documented] The Sep 2021 overhaul split MAL's "15 year old" genre list into three axes: Genres, Themes and Demographics. Demographics (Shounen, Seinen, etc.) stopped being genres. Records from before 2021, and old scrapes, mix all three in a single "genres" list.
2. [Documented] Genres are "Top-level story categories which apply to a very wide range of works" and are set by moderators only. Themes are categories that "easily contain 50-100+ well-known works". Themes were planned to be set by moderators and by user vote through list "Tags", with a hidden vote threshold that moderators can bypass or blacklist. Tags are user-defined only.
3. [Documented] Renames and removals in Part 1. Dementia became Avant Garde. Shounen Ai became Boys Love. Shoujo Ai became Girls Love. Thriller became Suspense. Yaoi became Boys Love plus Erotica, and Yuri became Girls Love plus Erotica. Magic was removed as a synonym of Fantasy. New genres: Award Winning (moderator-set, for works that won awards the MAL News Team announces), Erotica (between Ecchi and Hentai), Gourmet (includes drink; グルメ漫画) and Work Life (a Japanese genre about the work life of an occupation, not just a workplace setting). Psychological, Parody, Military, Music and similar moved to Themes.
4. [Documented] Part 1.5 (Mar 28, 2022) released the new themes, including Isekai, CGDCT and Iyashikei, and genre definitions shown on each genre page. MAL said it "systematically reviewed 1,200 themes from other website[s]" and "combed through ~8,000 entries" to seed themes, "and we are sure there are many still missing". At launch only 11 of 51 themes had definitions. Part 3, user voting on themes, was still "Coming Soon".
5. [Documented] Current taxonomy on anime.php (live, 2026-11-14) has 18 Genres: Action, Adventure, Avant Garde, Award Winning, Boys Love, Comedy, Drama, Fantasy, Girls Love, Gourmet, Horror, Mystery, Romance, Sci-Fi, Slice of Life, Sports, Supernatural, Suspense. There are 3 "Explicit Genres": Ecchi (831), Erotica (93), Hentai (1,654). There are 5 Demographics: Josei (162), Kids (7,129), Seinen (1,231), Shoujo (553), Shounen (2,347).

7. [Documented] Genres, themes and demographics share one ID space (/anime/genre/{id}). Kids is ID 15 and Shounen is ID 27, the same namespace as Action (1). A filter must know which IDs are demographics.
8. [Documented] Theme definitions can nest. The Iyashikei page says it "is a theme within the Slice of Life genre, and thus the conditions for both defintions must be met for the tag", and "Pets-themed stories are a subcategory of Iyashikei, so they are not double-tagged". The Isekai and Seinen pages showed no definition text on 2026-11-14.
9. [Documented] The theme set has grown since the 2022 launch: 51 themes at launch, 52 listed now. Newer IDs such as Crossdressing 81, Urban Fantasy 82 and Villainess 83 sit above the launch range. MAL itself said: "This will not be the last time we update the genres/themes."
10. [Documented] Part 1 said many entries' tags would be reshuffled after definitions were written, and Part 1.5 said "the genre tags on many anime/manga still need to be updated". Theme coverage of older titles can be incomplete.
11. [Reported] Forum replies (users) noted the mismatch between Kids (7,129 titles, far the largest demographic) and the other demographics. They also complained about the loss of Magic and the "Thriller" to "Suspense" rename. These are user opinions, not policy.
12. [Inferred] For the recommender, map MAL genres, themes and demographics as three separate fields. Hide Explicit Genres (Ecchi, Erotica, Hentai) unless the user opts in. Treat an absent theme as "unknown", not "no". When reading old data, translate Dementia, Thriller, Shounen Ai, Shoujo Ai, Yaoi, Yuri and Magic into the new names.
