## Learned points
1. [Documented] The tool's own description: it "Finds all related titles in the franchise for a specified anime and sorts them according to air dates". The ordering rule is release (air) order, not story chronology and not a curated "recommended" order.
2. [Documented] The URL path uses MAL anime IDs: /?/tools/watch_order/id/{MAL id}. id/1575 is MAL's Code Geass: Hangyaku no Lelouch and id/5081 is Bakemonogatari. Groups also have their own IDs (/?/tools/watch_order/group_id/{n}), and there are index pages for popular and large groups (watch_order_groups/type/popular and type/large).
3. [Documented] Each row links to MyAnimeList, AniList, Shikimori and Simkl for that title. The row's HTML attributes expose data-id (MAL id), data-anilist-id (sometimes empty, sometimes different from the MAL id, e.g. MAL 22297 maps to AniList 19603), data-type, data-eps, data-duration (seconds) and data-related, a JSON map from related MAL id to relation label.
4. [Documented] The relation labels in data-related match MAL's relation vocabulary: Sequel, Prequel, Side Story, Parent Story, Summary, Full Story, Alternative Version, Alternative Setting, Spin-Off, Character, Other. A manual cross-check on the MAL 1575 page found the same labels, for example Summary for the Recaps and Black Rebellion, and Alternative Setting for Boukoku no Akito.
5. [Documented] Every row shows the date range, type, episode count × duration and MAL score with voter count, e.g. "Oct 6, 2006 – Jul 29, 2007 | TV | 25ep × 24min. | ★8.71 (1,544,168)". Unannounced projects are listed as "Period unknown | Type unknown | 0ep" (Code Geass: Hoshi Oi no Aspal).
6. [Documented] Filters are type checkboxes, all checked by default: TV (1), Movie (3), OVA (2), ONA (5), Special (4), TV Special (9). Views are List and Timeline. Users can connect a personal anime list ("Your anime list source: Not connected") so the page shows their watch status.
7. [Documented] The group includes everything, with no separate main-story path. The Code Geass list mixes 1–2 minute "Omake Flash" shorts, picture dramas and recaps in with the two TV seasons, the Akito movies (Alternative Setting), the 2017–18 compilation movies (Alternative Version) and the 2024 ONA Dakkan no Rozé.
8. [Documented] Air order differs from story order. In Monogatari, Kizumonogatari I (Jan 8, 2016) is placed after Owarimonogatari (Oct 2015) because of release dates. MAL id 9260 is an older id, which suggests the film was registered long before release. The list shows titles' release dates, not the story timeline.
9. [Documented] Group boundaries are limited. The "Fate/stay night" group (id/356) has 16 entries covering stay night, Zero, UBW, Heaven's Feel and Emiya-san Chi no Kyou no Gohan. It does not include other Fate titles such as Fate/Apocrypha or Fate/Grand Order. (Their absence was observed on this page. Their existence on MAL was not checked here.) A watch-order group is therefore not the same as the whole franchise.
10. [Documented] The type shown follows MAL's types, including "TV Special" (for example Nekomonogatari: Kuro and the Owarimonogatari 2nd Season TV Specials).
11. [Inferred] For the recommender, chiaki is a fast key-free way to list related MAL ids in air order for a franchise and to get MAL to AniList id mappings. The "where to start" choice still has to be made from relation labels: drop Summary, Alternative Version, picture dramas and shorts, and keep Sequel/Prequel chains from the TV entry. Do not present chiaki order as the official watch order for franchises like Monogatari or Fate, where fans argue over the order.

## Limitations or bias
- It is a fan-run site with no documented API, terms or rate limits on the pages read. Scraping its HTML attributes is fragile.
- Data depends on MAL's relation graph and MAL's dates, including the MAL JST/preview-date rules (see S12). How group membership is decided is not documented.
- There is no FAQ or about page linked from the tool pages, so the data source is inferred from the ids and links (strongly MAL).
