## Learned points
1. [Documented] Operator: TMDB is "operated by TiVo Platform Technologies LLC". The licence is worldwide, non-exclusive, non-transferable, and non-sublicensable. It is conditioned on compliance with the general TMDB Terms of Use and on attribution.
2. [Documented] AI restriction (§1.C): you must not "Use the TMDB APIs or TMDB Content in connection with, including for training, a machine learning (ML) or artificial intelligence (AI) based Application." §1.A also reserves to TMDB the right to use content "for training, a machine learning or artificial intelligence based Application".
3. [Documented] §2.A lists as commercial use (requiring a separate written agreement, possibly with fees) any use "in connection with a 'destination' website, search engine, or interactive query-response system (including large language model (LLM), artificial intelligence, or any other machine learning based interactive query-response systems or chatbots)". It also covers "Training or validating a machine learning or artificial intelligence system ... collecting data sets containing TMDB Content for such training or validation".
4. [Inferred] A Claude Code recommender that queries TMDB and feeds the results to an LLM plausibly falls under the §1.C "in connection with ... AI based Application" restriction even for personal use. The terms make no personal-use exception for this clause. Treat TMDB as a source that needs the user's own judgement or written permission, not as freely usable. Flag this risk clearly to the reader; it is the main legal caveat among the title APIs.
5. [Documented] Caching limit: you must not "Cache, for longer than 6 months, any information obtained through or from TMDB or the TMDB APIs."
6. [Documented] Other restrictions: no cloaking of identity; no excessive bandwidth or degrading the service; no derivatives of the APIs or content; no image hosting for ads; no selling, leasing, or sublicensing; no use that misleads about the app's source.
7. [Documented] Attribution (§3): you must use the TMDB logo, less prominently than your own app's marks, without implying endorsement, and place this notice prominently: "This [website, program, service, application, product] uses TMDB and the TMDB APIs but is not endorsed, certified, or otherwise approved by TMDB." The developer FAQ (S3) gives a shorter variant ("This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.") and says to put it in an About/Credits section.
8. [Documented] Termination (§1.D): TMDB "may terminate the license at any time for any reason". On termination you must "promptly delete or otherwise purge all TMDB Content, including any cached content."
9. [Documented] No warranty (§6): TMDB does not warrant the APIs are "free of inaccuracies, errors" or "reliable, accurate, complete". Governing law is California (Santa Clara County).
10. [Documented] TMDB may change the terms by posting, and continued use means acceptance (§10.H).
11. [Documented] Logos page: "every application that uses our data or images is required to properly attribute TMDB as the source". Brand colours: primary #0d253f, secondary #01b4e4, tertiary #90cea1. Approved SVGs: Primary full/short/long (blue), Alt long/short (blue). The FAQ says the logo must not be recoloured, stretched, flipped, or rotated, and the name must be "TMDB" or "The Movie Database".
