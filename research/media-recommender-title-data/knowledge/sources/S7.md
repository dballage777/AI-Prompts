## Learned points
1. [Documented] `MediaRelation` is described as "Type of relation media has to its parent." The values and their descriptions are: ADAPTATION ("An adaption of this media into a different format"), PREQUEL ("Released before the relation"), SEQUEL ("Released after the relation"), PARENT ("The media a side story is from"), SIDE_STORY, CHARACTER ("Shares at least 1 character"), SUMMARY ("A shortened and summarized version"), ALTERNATIVE ("An alternative version of the same media"), SPIN_OFF ("An alternative version of the media with a different primary focus"), and OTHER.
2. [Documented] Some values are versioned. SOURCE ("The source material the media was adapted from"), COMPILATION and CONTAINS are "Version 2 only". SAME_UNIVERSE ("set in the same universe as another media") is "Version 3 only". COMPILATION and CONTAINS have no description.
3. [Documented] `MediaEdge` (inside `Media.relations`) has `node` (Media), `id`, and `relationType` with a `version` argument ("Provide 3 to use new version 3 of relation enum"). The other MediaEdge fields (characterRole, voiceActors, staffRole, isMainStudio, etc.) serve the character, staff and studio connections, not relations.
4. [Documented, live test] The relation version changes the labels you get. Without a version argument, Fullmetal Alchemist (2003, id 121) showed its source manga (30025) and its novel (34658) as `ADAPTATION`. With `relationType(version:2)`, the same edges came back as `SOURCE`. So in v1, ADAPTATION points in both directions and you have to check `node.type`/`format` to tell which side is the source.
5. [Documented, live test] FMA 2003 (id 121), queried with `relationType(version:2)`, returned: SEQUEL → 430 MOVIE 2005 "Shamballa wo Yuku Mono"; SIDE_STORY → 908 OVA 2006 "PREMIUM COLLECTION"; SUMMARY → 664 SPECIAL 2005 "Reflections"; ALTERNATIVE → 5114 TV 2009 "Hagane no Renkinjutsushi: FULLMETAL ALCHEMIST" (Brotherhood); SOURCE → 30025 MANGA and 34658 NOVEL. ALTERNATIVE therefore links the two separate retellings of one source, the 2003 anime and Brotherhood.
6. [Documented, live test] Bakemonogatari (id 5081, 2009), with version 2, returned: PREQUEL → 15689 "Nekomonogatari (Kuro)" (TV, 2012); SEQUEL → 11597 "Nisemonogatari" (TV, 2012); SIDE_STORY → 21520 "Koyomimonogatari" (ONA, 2016); OTHER → 106495 "Nisekoimonogatari" (ONA, 2015); SOURCE → 44893 NOVEL; CHARACTER → 54499 (a guidebook); ALTERNATIVE → 101311 MANGA "Bakemonogatari" and 143663 SPECIAL "Bakemonogatari PV".
7. [Inferred, from test 6] PREQUEL/SEQUEL do not always follow the release date as the enum text says. Nekomonogatari (Kuro), released in 2012, is marked PREQUEL to the 2009 Bakemonogatari because it takes place earlier in the story. A watch-order tool must not treat PREQUEL as "aired earlier", and SEQUEL chains can differ from both release order and story order.
8. [Inferred, from test 6] ALTERNATIVE is used loosely. It covers the manga version of a novel-sourced work and also a promo-video SPECIAL, not only alternate anime retellings. Filter on `node.type == ANIME` and `format` before treating ALTERNATIVE as "a different version you could watch instead".
9. [Inferred] Relations come one hop at a time from each entry. To build a whole franchise you have to walk SEQUEL/PREQUEL/SIDE_STORY/PARENT edges across several requests. With the current 30 requests/minute limit (see S2), keep walks shallow and batch them with aliases.
10. [Inferred] Rules for entry points and watch order: start from the entry with no anime PREQUEL among TV, MOVIE and ONA formats. Mark SUMMARY and COMPILATION entries as skippable recaps. Mark SIDE_STORY entries as optional extras. Present ALTERNATIVE anime as a choice between versions (for example FMA 2003 or Brotherhood), not as part of a sequence.

## Limitations or bias
- The enum descriptions are one line each. The docs do not define COMPILATION or CONTAINS, and they do not say whether PREQUEL/SEQUEL means release order or story order. The tests suggest story or editorial order is used.
- Relation edges are edited by the community and may be incomplete or uneven between entries. Only two franchises were tested.
- The version 3 relation labels (SAME_UNIVERSE) were not tested live.

## Best used for
- Building franchise graphs, telling sequels apart from recaps, side stories and alternate retellings, and finding the source material (version 2 SOURCE edges) to answer "is this adapted from a manga or novel".
