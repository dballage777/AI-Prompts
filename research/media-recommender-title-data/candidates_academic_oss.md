## Key facts noted

- The "1%-23%" invalid-title figure comes from Beyond Utility: "around 1%" for most LLMs and "around 23% in Beauty" for Mistral. Error types include omitting part of a title, combining titles, and misstating attributes. The authors say a mapping strategy is needed. https://arxiv.org/html/2411.00331v1
- Hallucination depends heavily on the catalog: OOD@10 is 0.6-2.7% on MovieLens-25M, 11.6-38.7% on Yelp, and 49.3-61.0% on Amazon Toys. Verbalized confidence does not track it, so self-reported confidence cannot serve as a validity check. The measured rate also moves by an order of magnitude depending on the string matcher, so the matcher must be validated. https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.10008
- Movie titles from GPT-3.5-turbo and GPT-4 matched IMDb 95.51% and 94.86% of the time (a lower bound on non-hallucinated titles). The open models scored 81.56% (BAIZE) and 86.98% (Vicuna). https://arxiv.org/html/2308.10053
- Catalog-constrained generation and retrieval eliminate out-of-domain items entirely (OOD@10 = 0 for all RecLM variants). https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.03336
- SRBench measured invalid-item rates up to 16.58% (Beauty) and 17.52% (Yelp) under few-shot prompting, and the authors call for content validation. https://arxiv.org/html/2604.09553
- Long-tail movie facts are weak. In Head-to-Tail's Movie domain (IMDb snapshot of 21 May 2023), GPT-4 scored 59.3% accuracy with 14.8% hallucination on head entities, versus 10.9% accuracy with 14.7% hallucination on tail entities. Over 99.9% of IMDb titles fall in the tail. https://arxiv.org/html/2308.10168v2
- Retrieval mostly improves accuracy on recently released movies (CRAG CRS). https://arxiv.org/html/2502.14137v1. Empirical knowledge boundaries also diverge from declared cutoffs (https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.12116). Together these mean any title from 2024 on should be verified live.
- The RAR unified movie corpus has 337,731 entries merged from IMDb, TMDB, MovieLens, and ReDial, covering about half of IMDb's movies as of 2025. https://arxiv.org/html/2604.04457v1
- Gracenote (industry press release, not peer-reviewed, June 2026) tested 2,600 movie and TV titles in 13 countries. An ungrounded LLM hallucinated all measured metadata for 506 titles (about 1 in 5). https://gracenote.com/newsroom/ungrounded-llm-fabricates-every-detail-for-nearly-1-in-5-movie-and-tv-titles-tested-new-gracenote-report-finds/
- Wikidata film properties are sparse: cost data exists for 60 of 2,676 films from 2020. https://arxiv.org/html/2207.00143
- Wikidata's anime coverage is low compared with the dedicated databases. A live SPARQL count on 2026-11-14 found 7,303 items with a MAL anime ID (P4086), and 7,151 of them also have an AniList ID (P8729). manami lists 30,570 MAL entries (week 27, 2026), so Wikidata covers roughly a quarter of them. Sources: https://query.wikidata.org/sparql and the manami README.
- ID mapping coverage:
  - manami (41,537 entries, 65% reviewed) has entry counts per provider: MAL 30,570; anime-planet 26,674; Kitsu 22,139; anisearch 21,105; AniList 20,691; AniDB 14,517; Simkl 14,494; ANN 12,619; LiveChart 12,278. Its dataset files moved to GitHub Releases after the 2025-25 update. https://raw.githubusercontent.com/manami-project/anime-offline-database/HEAD/README.md
  - Fribb adds TMDB (tv/movie), TVDB with season and episode offsets, and IMDb. It notes the themoviedb_id.tv field is a scalar while the movie field is an array. https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Fribb/anime-lists/HEAD/README.md
  - Kometa maps AniDB to TVDB series, IMDb, MAL, AniList, TMDB movie, and TMDB show, regenerated daily. https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Kometa-Team/Anime-IDs/HEAD/README.md
- AniList: the documented limit is 90 requests per minute, but a warning says the API is currently degraded and limited to 30 per minute. The terms prohibit hoarding, mass collection, and backup use, and allow free non-commercial use. Media.isAdult flags 18+ titles. Each MediaTag has a rank (0-100), spoiler flags, and isAdult. MediaRelation types (PREQUEL, SEQUEL, SIDE_STORY, ADAPTATION, SOURCE, and others) support watch-order and adaptation-status checks. https://docs.anilist.co/guide/rate-limiting ; https://docs.anilist.co/guide/terms-of-use ; https://docs.anilist.co/reference/enum/mediarelation ; https://docs.anilist.co/reference/object/mediatag
- TMDB: the legacy limit of 40 requests per 10 seconds was disabled on 16 Dec 2019. The current upper limit is "somewhere in the 40 requests per second range," and clients should respect HTTP 429. Daily ID exports (valid IDs plus adult, video, and popularity fields, at files.tmdb.org, starting around 07:00 UTC) enable offline existence checks. The site offers llms.txt and .md page versions for agents. https://developer.themoviedb.org/docs/rate-limiting.md ; https://developer.themoviedb.org/docs/daily-id-exports.md
- IMDb datasets are daily-refreshed TSVs that use \N for nulls. title.akas carries localized titles by region. They are licensed for personal and non-commercial use only, and the backing source changed on 18 Mar 2024. https://data.imdb.com/non-commercial-datasets/
- IGDB allows 4 requests per second with up to 8 concurrent requests (HTTP 429 above that). It has Age Rating and Franchise/Collection endpoints. https://api-docs.igdb.com/
- Jikan is a scraper for read-only MAL data. Its v4 line is end of life and v5.0.0 is current. https://raw.githubusercontent.com/jikan-me/jikan/master/readme.md
- RestBench-TMDB has 54 TMDB APIs and 100 human-annotated instructions with gold paths, so it is a ready test set for an agent's TMDB tool use. https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.06624
