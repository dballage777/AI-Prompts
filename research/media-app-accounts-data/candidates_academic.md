# Candidates: academic and scholarly, plus standards and government (discovery batch)

Topic: backend, accounts and data sync for a small consumer React Native + Expo app (Supabase vs. Firebase vs. Cloudflare D1/Durable Objects; Sign in with Apple and Google sign-in; RLS and security rules; taste profiles, seen lists, watchlists; offline sync; account deletion; small-scale cost).
Worker: academic + standards/government batch. Run date: 2026-11-25. All URLs were opened with curl (UA "MediaAppResearch/0.1"). Where a line says "Full text open", the PDF or HTML was fetched and read. Venue details come from the opened page unless marked "venue not shown on page".
Format: name (authors, year) | canonical URL | type | why it matters (concrete finding) | how found | date checked | status
