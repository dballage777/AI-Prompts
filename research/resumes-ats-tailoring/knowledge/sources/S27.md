## Learned points
1. Dates: use MM/YYYY or "Month Year" consistently (correct: "Jun 2020 - Present" or "06/2020 - Present"; incorrect: "Jun. 2020 - Present", "2020/06 - Present"). Rationale given: ATS parse dates to calculate employment duration and inconsistent formats can cause experience to be miscalculated or ignored. — [Documented]
2. Acronyms: include both the spelled-out term and acronym, e.g., "Search Engine Optimization (SEO)" rather than "SEO" alone; page claims Taleo and Lever may not recognize acronyms not spelled out. — [Documented] (vendor-derived claim, no evidence shown)
3. Graphics/characters: avoid images, graphics and non-standard bullet characters; claims Lever and iCIMS can struggle to parse them. Use plain text and simple bullets (• or -). — [Documented]
4. Lines sparingly; avoid tables (may not parse correctly); use color minimally with high text/background contrast. — [Documented]
5. Headers/footers: keep critical info (contact details) out of headers and footers; put name and contact at top of the main body because ATS "typically do not read" headers/footers. — [Documented]
6. Margins: 1 inch on all sides; may reduce to 0.75 inch if space is tight but avoid crowding. — [Documented]
7. Fonts: standard fonts such as Arial, Times New Roman, Calibri; body 10–12 pt, headings 14–16 pt. Notes Lever and Greenhouse show resumes exactly as submitted, so human readability matters. — [Documented]
8. Section headings: use conventional headings ("Work Experience," "Education," "Skills," "Certifications"); custom headings like "Professional Journey," "Learning Achievements," and even "Core Competencies" are listed as incorrect. — [Documented]
9. Recommended sections and content: Contact Information (full name, phone, email, LinkedIn); Summary or Objective (brief statement of goals and key qualifications); Work Experience in reverse chronological order with job titles, company names, locations, dates; Education (degrees, institutions, graduation dates); Skills (relevant, focused on those in the job description); Certifications. — [Documented]
10. File type: "Prioritize PDF formats"; says most ATS can accept and prefer PDF though some accept .docx; PDF must not be image-based; always follow the job posting's file-format instructions. — [Documented]
11. Proofread multiple times; suggests Grammarly or Hemingway Editor; errors can cause ATS and humans to misinterpret qualifications. — [Documented]
12. Keyword tailoring is implied only via Skills ("focusing on those mentioned in the job description"); the page gives no guidance on keyword density, stuffing, or honesty. — [Inferred]
13. The PDF-first advice conflicts with other sources in this set (e.g., S28 Huntr reports Workday parses .docx with fewer errors); the safe rule is to follow the employer's stated file format. — [Inferred]
