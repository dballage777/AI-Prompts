## Learned points
1. The "ATS Considerations" block lists: no images, logos, or colorful designs; do not use a template; no headers or footers; quantify when possible; no acronyms unless well known in the field; convert to PDF each time you upload. — Documented
2. The checklist recommends PDF for ATS uploads ("Convert to a PDF each time you upload"). This conflicts with sources that prefer .docx for some ATS. The checklist gives no reason or ATS-specific basis for its choice. — Documented (the conflict with other sources is Inferred)
3. "Do not use a template" appears twice, the second time as "Don’t use a template to format." It suggests building the resume in a plain document rather than in prebuilt designed templates. No reason is given. — Documented
4. Header block: name large and bold at the top (16–24 pt), with contact information below or beside it (City, State, Zip, Email, Phone). Note that the "no headers or footers" rule means the name should be in the document body, not in a Word header field. — Documented (the header-field interpretation is Inferred)
5. Optional Objective or Summary of Qualifications under the contact information. An Objective should be specific (position title, company, time frame). Both should name the candidate's skills and state what the candidate offers the employer, not what the employer offers the candidate. — Documented
6. Education: degree and major (the degree may come first), graduation date only (for example, "May 2015"), GPA if 3.0 or higher (required for engineering and technical programs), study abroad if applicable. List only institutions that granted a degree. — Documented
7. Optional Relevant Coursework: 4–8 advanced, position-related courses, titles only, "if needed to fill the page." — Documented
8. Experience sections (Internship, Work, Leadership, and so on) in reverse chronological order. Each entry gives Position, Company, City/State, and Dates (for example, "May 2011 – July 2013"). At least 3 bullets per position. Each bullet starts with an action verb in the correct tense, uses numbers (timeframe, money, and so on), and needs no period. — Documented
9. Possible extra sections: Class Project Experience, College Activities, Volunteer Experience, Honors and Awards (with dates), Computer Skills, Language Proficiencies, and field-specific sections (Related Experience, Lab Techniques). — Documented
10. Length: one page for undergraduates; "two plus pages" for graduate level and above. — Documented
11. Layout: dates on the right side of the page. Consistent use of italics and bold. 10–12 pt traditional font (it lists "Ariel" [sic], Times New Roman). Margins no less than 0.5 inch. — Documented
12. Tailoring: tailor information to the target position and put the most relevant information near the top. — Documented
13. Do not write "References available upon request." Make a separate reference page with the same header as the resume. Leave out high school unless the user is a first-year student, it relates to the position, or it is needed to fill the page. — Documented
14. Implication for a .docx/.pdf generator: single-column body text, no images or graphics, no Word header or footer for contact details, plain non-template styling, a PDF export for upload, and acronyms spelled out unless standard in the field. — Inferred

## Limitations / bias
- This is a terse checklist for SIU students. Its ATS rules come with no reasons, evidence, or named ATS products, and the PDF-only advice is not qualified.
- It contains typos ("Ariel," "Objection") and outdated example dates (2011–2015), which suggest some content was carried over from older versions.
- Advice such as adding coursework or high school "to fill the page" applies to students only.
## Best for
- A compact, current (2025) checklist of ATS-safe formatting rules and section order that a bot can apply when generating .docx or .pdf resumes.
