## Learned points
1. Source of the tips: two recruiters, a staff recruiting manager at a law firm (Covington & Burling) and a talent acquisition senior associate at PwC, speaking at an American University Professional Studies webinar. — [Reported]
2. Premise: AI in applicant tracking systems may screen out resumes before a human sees them. This claim is in tension with S16's 2025 recruiter interviews. — [Reported]
3. Read the job post carefully and match your top skills and qualifications to the listed requirements. — [Reported] (summary-only)
4. Include keywords from the job description so you aren't "immediately cut out of the hiring process." — [Reported]
5. Look for more "buzzwords" on the company website and in its leaders' speeches and public comments. The bot should add these only where they truthfully describe the user. — [Reported]; caveat [Inferred]
6. Avoid charts and visual displays, which don't work well in ATS platforms. — [Reported]
7. File type: submit a Word document or PDF so recruiters can open it easily. — [Reported] (summary-only)
8. Research the company and mention some of its background. Recruiters appreciate candidates who have "done their homework." — [Reported] (summary-only)
9. Application tactics: apply within the first 24 hours; keep a log so you don't apply twice or send duplicate resumes to one company; check company career sites as well as job boards; then message a recruiter on LinkedIn. — [Reported] (summary-only)
10. Market data it cites: ResearchAndMarkets.com estimated the worldwide ATS market would reach $3.3B by 2027 (7.1% annual growth), and it reports funding rounds for Fetcher ($27M Series B) and Fountain, an hourly-worker ATS ($85M Series C). — [Reported] (summary-only)
11. The article does not cover using generative AI to write resumes, authenticity, or auto-apply tools. The "age of AI" in the title refers to AI in employer screening. — [Documented]

## Limitations / bias
- It is from 2022, before generative AI tools were widely used, so it is outside the 2024+ scope. Use it as background only.
- It reflects the opinions of only two recruiters from a webinar, with no data on ATS behavior.
- The "screened out before a human sees it" framing is not evidenced, and it conflicts with S16.
- I read it through a summarizer, so quotes were not checked against the raw HTML.
## Best for
- Basic, widely repeated tailoring advice (mirror job-post keywords, no charts or graphics, Word or PDF), with a note that it predates generative AI.
