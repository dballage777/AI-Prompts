## Learned points
1. Etsy is testing a way for buyers to turn eligible digital files they bought on Etsy's website into printed items. Eligible listings may be included automatically (opt-out, not opt-in). — Documented
2. After buying an eligible digital file, the buyer may see an option to order a printed item through a separate checkout on Etsy. They choose options, pick the file, preview it, and place a separate order. — Documented
3. A third-party print provider, Ventur Editions, sells and fulfills the printed item from its own shop. The printed item has its own listing and reviews, and Ventur Editions handles support for problems with that order. — Documented
4. Eligibility conditions: the shop is based in the US or Canada; the seller does not already offer a physical version of the design or use their own print-on-demand integration; and the file meets the print partner's requirements for high-quality printing. — Documented
5. The article does not say whether the original digital seller earns anything from the printed order. — Documented absence / Inferred implication
6. The opt-out is shop-wide, not per listing: Shop Manager > Settings > Options > "Allow buyers to purchase digital prints" > Disabled. — Documented
7. Opting out removes the print offer shown after purchase, but it does not stop buyers from using Ventur Editions' services on their own with files they downloaded. — Documented
8. For coloring-page sellers: decide whether you want buyers' physical prints made by a third party. If you plan to sell your own printed coloring books on Etsy, offering a physical version makes the design ineligible anyway. Your digital-file license terms (for example, personal use only) are the main tool for limiting reprinting. — Inferred
9. The print partner's "requirements for high-quality printing" (resolution, size) are not listed in the article. — Documented absence
