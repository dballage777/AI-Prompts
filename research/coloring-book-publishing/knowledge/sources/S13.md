## Learned points
1. Title field must contain only the actual title as it appears on the cover. Prohibited: repeated generic keywords (e.g. "notebook," "journal," "gifts," "books"), unauthorized references to other titles/authors or trademarked terms, false implied contributors, sales rank ("bestselling"), promotions ("free"), punctuation-only, "unknown"/"n/a"/"blank"/"none", HTML. Documented
2. Title + subtitle together must be fewer than 200 characters; subtitle follows the same rules as the title. Documented
3. For print books, the title must appear on the cover (spine or front) and match the setup metadata; if it appears in the manuscript it must match. Documented
4. Multi-story books need "Collection," "Compilation," or "Series" in title and cover. Documented
5. Public domain and low-content books (e.g. notebooks, journals) are not eligible for a series. Series names follow title rules; series numbers are digits only. Documented
6. Description may NOT contain: pornographic/obscene content; phone numbers, mailing/email addresses, URLs; reviews, quotes or testimonials; requests for reviews; ads or watermarks; time-sensitive info; price/availability/other ordering info; spoilers; keywords or tag phrases. HTML is allowed (supported tags). Documented
7. Author: pen names allowed if they don't impair buying decisions; if similar to a popular author, consider a pen name, middle name or initial; no HTML. Documented
8. Contributors: list illustrators, editors etc. only if they worked on that book; no keywords or HTML; all contributors required for public domain books. Documented
9. Publisher field (eBook only, optional): no references to other publishers, Amazon, Kindle/KDP, or websites. Documented
10. Keywords: phrases of 2-3 words recommended; prohibited: other authors, their books, sales rank, "free"/promotions, anything unrelated to content, HTML; misleading keywords not tolerated. Documented
11. Primary audience: you must answer Yes/No to "Sexually Explicit Images or Title"; Yes makes the book ineligible for Children's categories and hidden from general search. Documented
12. Reading age is optional, but without an appropriate reading age a children's book may not be eligible for selected children's categories or age-specific search. Relevant to kids' coloring books. Documented (application: Inferred)
13. Categories: choose up to three, based on primary audience and marketplace; irrelevant/manipulative categories not tolerated. Documented
14. Cover: title, subtitle, author and series on the cover must match metadata; covers closely resembling another book's layout, color scheme, fonts and/or images are not allowed. Documented
15. ISBN: required for paperback/hardcover except low-content books; KDP offers a free ISBN or you can use your own; an ISBN printed in the manuscript must match setup. Documented
16. Prompt rule: an AI assistant drafting titles/descriptions/keywords should be told to avoid keyword-stuffed titles ("coloring book for kids gifts…" repetition), trademarked characters/brands, "bestselling"/"free", URLs and review quotes in descriptions. Inferred
