## Learned points
1. Use Lulu's Adobe Job Options with Adobe Acrobat Pro; template bundles include interior and cover export presets. — Documented
2. All interior content in a single PDF, including copyright page and blank pages. — Documented
3. Interior: images 300 ppi; fonts embedded or outlined; flatten transparent layers and vector objects; single-page layout. — Documented
4. Page order: page 1 prints on the right-hand side; odd pages right, even pages left. — Documented (relevant to single-sided coloring layouts: art on odd pages, blanks on even — Inferred)
5. Bleed 0.125 in; example 6 x 9 in book → PDF pages 6.25 x 9.25 in. — Documented
6. Interior safety margin 0.50 in for all content; gutter minimum 0.20 in on the inner edge. — Documented
7. No trim or bleed marks; no security/password protection (interior and cover). — Documented
8. Cover: one-piece PDF (back, spine, front) as single-page spread; images 300–600 PPI; rasterize vector images; fonts embedded/outlined; flatten transparency; spine width depends on page count (Spine Width Calculator PDF). — Documented
9. Cover bleed 0.125 in; cover safety margin minimum 0.25 in (0.75 in for Hardcover Casewrap). — Documented (differs from S20's 0.5 in cover safety margin)
10. Color: printers use sRGB and CMYK; prefer sRGB; keep CMYK if file was built in CMYK; changing color space after finalizing risks variance. — Documented
11. Solid black prints at 100% with no other colors; rich black TAC ≤270%; avoid tints below 20%. — Documented
12. B&W images in a color book should be grayscale, gamma 2.2–2.4. — Documented
13. Color variance between orders/printers is normal; recommended sRGB + GRACoL ICC; report out-of-tolerance variance within 30 days of shipment via support form. — Documented
