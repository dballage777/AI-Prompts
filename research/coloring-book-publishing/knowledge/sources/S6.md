## Learned points
1. Paperback ink/paper options: black ink + 50–61 lb (74–90 GSM) cream; black ink + 50–61 lb (74–90 GSM) white; black ink + 45 lb (60 GSM) groundwood (new; paperback only); premium color + 60–71 lb (88–105 GSM) white; standard color + 50–61 lb (74–90 GSM) white. — Documented
2. Hardcover options: black/cream, black/white, premium color/white (same weights); groundwood and standard color not available for hardcover. Paper weight varies by printing location. — Documented
3. Color ink books print on white paper only; black ink books can use cream, white or groundwood. — Documented
4. Choose a color ink option if any images must print in color; color costs more. A black-and-white and a color version must be set up as separate books. — Documented
5. Ink type is locked after publication (ISBN requirements): you cannot switch between standard color, premium color and black-and-white. — Documented
6. Black-ink paperbacks can switch paper after publication: cream <-> white, cream <-> groundwood, white <-> groundwood; may require a new cover (spine width changes with page count/paper); updates take up to 72 hours. No paper switching for color titles. — Documented
7. Groundwood printing costs are "approximately 5% lower per page" than cream/white. — Documented
8. Coloring-book relevance: the lightest paper is groundwood at 45 lb (60 GSM), versus 50–61 lb (74–90 GSM) white; heavier paper is only available via premium color (60–71 lb / 88–105 GSM). Bleed-through from markers is a known concern for coloring books, but this page does not discuss it. — Documented (weights) / Inferred (relevance)
9. KDP recommends ordering a proof copy before publication to check ink and paper. — Documented
10. Default trim in Print Options is 6" x 9" (regular). Large trim = more than 6.12 in (155.5 mm) wide or more than 9 in (228.6 mm) tall; regular and large trims have different printing costs. — Documented
11. Custom paperback trim: width 4"–8.5" (10.16–21.59 cm); height 6"–11.69" (15.24–29.69 cm). — Documented
12. Hardcover trims: 5.5x8.5, 6x9, 6.14x9.21, 7x10, 8.25x11; 75–550 pages for black/white, black/cream, premium color; standard color not available. — Documented
13. Covers printed on 80 lb (220 GSM) white stock, glossy or matte. Glossy: shiny, "typical for textbooks, children's books, and nonfiction"; matte: minimal sheen, typical for fiction. Hardcovers are case laminate. — Documented
14. Standard color distribution for RTL paperbacks is limited to Amazon.co.jp. KDP describes itself as manufacture-on-demand; links to EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR) compliance info. — Documented
