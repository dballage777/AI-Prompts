## Learned points
1. Interior must be one PDF with ALL contents except the cover (title, copyright, intended blank pages), single-page layout (not spreads). — Documented
2. Ink options: Standard B&W, Premium B&W, Standard Color, Premium Color. Paper options: 60# Uncoated White, 60# Uncoated Cream, 80# Coated White. — Documented
3. Guide's pairings: 60# uncoated + B&W for few/no graphics; "80# Coated White with Premium Black & White ink" for high-contrast grayscale images; 60# uncoated white + Standard Color for light color elements; Premium Color on 80# coated for photos and full bleed. — Documented
4. Resolution: 72 PPI = screen; 150 PPI = minimum for simple print graphics; 300 PPI = ideal for complex/detailed print graphics. Final interior spec: images 300 PPI minimum, not exceeding 600 PPI. — Documented
5. Color: RGB documents fine; use sRGB (IEC61966-2.1); if already CMYK, stay CMYK (profile Coated GRACoL 2006). Solid black prints 100% K; rich black TAC never above 270%; tints at 20% or less are hard to control; B&W images in grayscale with gamma 2.2–2.4. — Documented
6. Bleed: 0.125 in / 3.175 mm on all sides. If nothing extends to the page edge, you can submit at trim size and Lulu adds bleed automatically; full bleed only when art extends to the edge. — Documented
7. Trim/file sizes relevant to coloring books: US Letter 8.5 x 11 in (with bleed 8.75 x 11.25 in); Square 8.5 x 8.5 in (8.75 x 8.75 in); Small Square 7.5 x 7.5 in (7.75 x 7.75 in); A4 8.27 x 11.69 in (8.52 x 11.94 in); US Letter Landscape 11 x 8.5 in (11.25 x 8.75 in). Full table covers 17 sizes. — Documented
8. Gutter (add to inside safety margin, spine side only), by page count: <60: 0 in (total inside 0.5 in); 61–150: 0.125 in (0.625 in); 151–400: 0.5 in (1 in); 400–600: 0.625 in (1.125 in); >600: 0.75 in (1.25 in). Coil bound and saddle stitch need no gutter. — Documented
9. Page minimums: paperback 32 interior pages; hardcover 24 interior pages. — Documented
10. Paperback spine width = (pages / 444) + 0.06 in, or (pages / 17.48) + 1.524 mm. Hardcover uses a table (e.g., 24–84 pages = 0.25 in). Coil and saddle stitch need no spine calc. — Documented
11. Spine text: none if 80 pages or fewer; keep ≥0.125 in clearance each side; trimming tolerance 0.125 in toward front/back cover. — Documented
12. Cover: single-page spread (back + spine + front); 300–600 PPI; 0.125 in bleed; 0.5 in / 12.7 mm safety margin; no trim/bleed marks; no password protection. Use Lulu's custom template (from Pricing Calculator or generated after interior upload). — Documented
13. Interior safety margin minimum 0.5 in; gutter margin minimum 0.2 in (the PDF prints "0.2 in/2.08 mm", an apparent unit error; 0.2 in = 5.08 mm). — Documented (error noted: Inferred)
14. Lulu scans uploads, auto-fixes small issues, alerts on big ones; download and review print-ready files before finishing. Adobe Job Options presets supplied (InDesign, Photoshop, Distiller instructions). — Documented
15. Proofing: "strongly recommend" buying one copy before selling; common file errors are low resolution, transparencies, unembedded fonts. — Documented
16. Internal inconsistency: guideline 9 says use "Premium Color ink and 80# White Uncoated paper" for heavy ink coverage, but the paper list has no 80# uncoated stock (only 80# Coated White). — Inferred
