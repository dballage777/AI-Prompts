## Learned points
1. The cover must be ONE PDF containing back cover, spine and front cover as a single image. Cover Creator is the alternative for simple designs. — Documented
2. File size: covers over 650MB won't convert; 40MB or less recommended. — Documented
3. Add 0.125" (3.2 mm) bleed to top, bottom and outside edges. Cover Width = Bleed + Back Cover Width + Spine Width + Front Cover Width + Bleed; Cover Height = Bleed + Trim Height + Bleed. KDP's cover calculator and template generator outputs a PNG/PDF template showing full cover, front cover, safe area, bleed, margin, spine, spine safe area, spine margin, barcode margin. — Documented
4. Spine width per page: white x 0.002252" (0.0572 mm); cream x 0.0025" (0.0635 mm); premium color x 0.002347" (0.0596 mm); standard color x 0.002252" (0.0572 mm). Groundwood factor not given here (S4 gives 0.00235"). — Documented
5. Spine text: page says both "at least 79 pages" and "more than 79 pages"; Cover Creator needs 80 pages. Spine text on a book under 79 pages causes rejection. Leave at least 0.0625" (1.6 mm) on either side of spine text; allow 0.0625" (1.6 mm) fold-line variance. — Documented
6. Coloring-book relevance: many coloring books are under 79–80 pages, so they cannot carry spine text; safest is to add spine text only at 80+ pages. — Inferred
7. Backgrounds and images must extend 0.125" (3.2 mm) past the trim line. Borders are not recommended (0.125" / 3.2 mm production variance); if used, borders must cover at least 0.25" (6.4 mm) inside the trim line, and white borders below the minimum width may be rejected. — Documented
8. Spine shift "up to 0.0125" (3.2mm)" is acceptable variance; avoid hard edges on the spine. Note the page's inch and mm values disagree (3.2 mm = 0.125", not 0.0125"). — Documented (observed typo)
9. Images: CMYK recommended; sized at 100%, flattened to one layer, minimum 300 DPI. — Documented
10. Text: at least 0.125" (3.2 mm) inside trim lines; no front/back text into the spine area; minimum 7-point font; ensure contrast with background. — Documented
11. Cover text must exactly match the book details entered at setup (title, author name, edition, ISBN). Low-content books published with "Publish without an ISBN" don't need ISBN info on the cover. — Documented
12. Barcode optional; KDP auto-places one on the back cover if you omit it. For RTL books, front cover is left of spine and barcode lower-left of back cover. — Documented
13. Color: don't include color profiles (removed automatically, can give unexpected results); no spot colors (e.g., Pantone) — convert to CMYK; avoid mixing color spaces; some color variance is within print-on-demand tolerance. — Documented
14. Pre-publication checklist: save as PDF; flatten transparencies; embed fonts; remove crop marks, color bars, template text, PDF creation guides, software references; 300 DPI minimum; optimize PDF; remove file security (locked/encrypted files fail); check for missing or excessive blank pages; consider ordering a proof copy. — Documented
15. Worked example: 8.5x11 B/W white, 100 pages -> spine 0.2252"; full cover 0.125 + 8.5 + 0.2252 + 8.5 + 0.125 = 17.4752" wide x 11.25" tall. Spine text is allowed at 100 pages, but after 0.0625" clearance on each side only about 0.1" of the 0.2252" spine is usable. — Inferred (arithmetic)
