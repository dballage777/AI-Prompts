## Learned points
1. Definitions: trim size = the printed book's width and height; bleed = printed objects extending past the trim line so they reach the page edge; margins = three outside margins (top, bottom, side) plus one inside margin (gutter). Set trim size before margins, because margins depend on page count and bleed. — Documented
2. Most common US trim size is 6" x 9" (152.4 x 228.6 mm), a "regular" trim. Large trim = more than 6.12 in (155.5 mm) wide or more than 9 in (228.6 mm) tall; large trims have different printing costs. — Documented
3. Paperback trim sizes (kdp.amazon.com) and page-count ranges, columns = black/white paper | black/cream | black/groundwood | standard color/white | premium color/white:
   - 5x8, 5.06x7.81, 5.25x8, 5.5x8.5, 6x9: 24–828 | 24–776 | 24–812 | 72–600 | 24–828
   - Large: 6.14x9.21, 6.69x9.61, 7x10, 7.44x9.69, 7.5x9.25, 8x10: same ranges as above
   - 8.25x6 and 8.25x8.25: 24–800 | 24–750 | 24–784 | 72–600 | 24–800
   - 8.5x8.5 and 8.5x11: 24–590 | 24–550 | 24–578 | 72–600 | 24–590
   - 8.27x11.69 (A4): 24–780 | 24–730 | 24–764 | Not available | 24–590 — Documented
4. Coloring-book relevance: the popular 8.5" x 11" (21.59 x 27.94 cm) paperback is a LARGE trim with a max of 590 pages on white paper (black ink); minimum is 24 pages for black ink, 72 for standard color. — Documented (table) / Inferred (relevance)
5. Hardcover trims (kdp.amazon.com): 5.5x8.5, 6x9, 6.14x9.21, 7x10, 8.25x11; 75–550 pages for black/white, black/cream and premium color; groundwood and standard color "Not available" for hardcover. Hardcover not available on kdp.amazon.co.jp; standard color unavailable for Amazon.co.jp. — Documented
6. Japan (kdp.amazon.co.jp) paperback trims listed separately (e.g., 4.06x7.17 through 8.27x11.69), mostly 24–828 B/W white; 8.27x10.12 and 8.27x11.69 cap at 780 (white). — Documented
7. All KDP covers require bleed; interior bleed is optional. Bleed is only supported for fixed-format files such as print-ready PDFs. — Documented
8. Pages are trimmed by cutting 0.125" (3.2 mm) from top, bottom and outside edges; bleed objects must extend 0.125" (3.2 mm) past the trim. — Documented
9. Bleed page-size formula: page height = trim height + 0.125" x 2; page width = trim width + 0.125" (single side, since the inside/spine edge is not trimmed). Metric: +3.2 mm x 2 for height, +3.2 mm for width. Example: 6x9 trim -> 6.125" x 9.25". 8.5x11 -> 8.625" x 11.25" (21.89 x 28.54 cm). 8.5x8.5 -> 8.625" x 8.75". 8x10 -> 8.125" x 10.25". — Documented
10. "Even if just one page in your interior requires bleed, then your entire file should be set up with bleed." Choose bleed whenever images, backgrounds or illustrations should reach the page edge. — Documented
11. Minimum outside (top, bottom, outside) margins: at least 0.25 in (6.4 mm) without bleed; at least 0.375 in (9.6 mm) with bleed. Top/bottom/outside margins need not be equal as long as each meets the minimum. — Documented
12. Minimum inside (gutter) margin by page count: 24–150 pages 0.375" (9.6 mm); 151–300 0.5" (12.7 mm); 301–500 0.625" (15.9 mm); 501–700 0.75" (19.1 mm); 701–828 0.875" (22.3 mm). If software has separate "inside margin" and "gutter" fields, enter the same value in both. — Documented
13. Wrong sizing can cause rejection on upload or print-quality problems. KDP offers downloadable Word templates per trim size (with or without sample content) and a calculator; Word/Pages guides "may not be up to date." — Documented
14. Practical implication for a coloring book (e.g., 8.5x11, under 150 pages, full-bleed art): PDF page 8.625" x 11.25", keep line art at least 0.375" from top/bottom/outside and 0.375" from the gutter (bleed outside margin and 24–150 gutter happen to be equal). Single-sided coloring pages still count toward page count. — Inferred
15. Inconsistency: the "Key terms" paragraph says "more than 6.12 inches (155.5 mm) or more than 9 inches (228.6 mm) in height" (omits "in width"); the Trim Size section states it correctly. The bleed table lists 8.25" x 11" (a hardcover-only trim in the trim tables). One bleed example's metric conversion (6.125 x 9.25 in = "15.54 x 23.46 cm") differs from the table's "15.56 x 23.50 cm". — Documented (observed on page)
