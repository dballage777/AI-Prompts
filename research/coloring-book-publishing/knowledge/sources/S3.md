## Learned points
1. Definition: a low-content book has minimal or no interior content, is generally repetitive, and is designed to be filled in by the user. Documented
2. KEY: coloring books are listed under "Not Generally Low-Content" (with puzzle books); activity books like these "generally do not feature repetitive content on each page." Exceptions possible. So a normal coloring book is set up as a regular print book, not low-content. Documented
3. Low-content examples: notebooks, planners, diaries/journals, prompt journals, log books, coupon books, score card templates, crafting templates (scrapbook paper, ephemera books), blank sheet music. Documented
4. Low-content books do not require an ISBN; options are your own ISBN or publish without one; they are NOT eligible for the free KDP ISBN. Documented
5. Release Date (pre-order scheduling) is not currently offered for low-content books. Documented
6. Setup: Bookshelf > + Create new title or series > Create paperback/hardcover; under Categories check the "Low-content" box; not selecting it (or selecting a different box) for a low-content book means the book "will be rejected." Documented
7. Without an ISBN (or without your own barcode), KDP places a barcode in a 2" (50.8 mm) by 1.2" (30.5 mm) white box in the lower right of the back cover; keep text/images out of that area or the file may be rejected. Documented
8. ISBN choice cannot be changed after publishing; you must unpublish and republish. Documented
9. Expanded Distribution is not supported for low-content books, whichever ISBN option is used. Documented
10. Look Inside / Read Sample and back-cover thumbnail are not supported for low-content books without an ISBN; KDP suggests buying an ISBN (Bowker or local agency) or using A+ Content to show interior images. Documented
11. Low-content books cannot be part of a series. Documented
12. Transparency codes are being retired and are not available for books created after November 2025. Documented
13. Hardcover publishing is not supported on kdp.amazon.co.jp. Documented
14. Implication: because a coloring book is not low-content, it uses the standard print path (ISBN required, free KDP ISBN available, Expanded Distribution and series available). Inferred (cross-check with S13: "you need an ISBN to publish a paperback or hardcover (except for low-content books)")
## Exact quotes
- "This does not typically include activity books, such as puzzle books or coloring books, which generally do not feature repetitive content on each page." — https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GGE5T76TWKA85DJM
- "Under Categories you need to check the Low-content box. Please note that if you do not select this box, or select a different box, your book will be rejected." — https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GGE5T76TWKA85DJM
- "Expanded Distribution is not supported for low-content books, regardless of the ISBN option you select." — https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/GGE5T76TWKA85DJM
