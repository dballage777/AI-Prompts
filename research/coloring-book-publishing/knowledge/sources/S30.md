## Learned points
1. Popular formats with "Starting at" prices: US Letter 8.5 x 11 in (216 x 279 mm) Paperback, Premium Black & White, 60# Uncoated White — 4.28 USD; Square 8.5 x 8.5 in (216 x 216 mm) Paperback, Premium B&W, 60# Uncoated White — 4.28 USD; US Letter 8.5 x 11 in Coil Bound, Standard B&W, 60# Uncoated White — 7.14 USD. — Documented (starting print prices; page count basis not shown)
2. Coil binding is presented as lay-flat "for easy coloring". — Documented
3. Images: 300 PPI (refers to PDF Creation Settings, S24). — Documented
4. FAQ paper/ink recommendation: "Standard Color on 60# uncoated" for "the roughest possible texture"; "Standard Color on 80# coated" for "a silkier, smoother surface" that accepts markers, crayon, and colored pencil. Lulu advises test-printing several combinations. — Documented
5. Bleed-through: print pages on one side only to prevent show-through; with colored pencils or crayons show-through is "unlikely", but single-sided is the only prevention. — Documented
6. Coated vs uncoated: 80# coated has a clay surface treatment; uncoated paper can darken and mute color photos. Line art "prints perfectly in Standard Color or Black & White" and is most economical. — Documented
7. Lulu uses a global printer network, so minor variance in ink and paper is possible. — Documented
8. Sales channels: Lulu Bookstore; own site via Shopify, Wix, WooCommerce integrations; Direct Buy Button (embed or link, social, email, QR); Print API; Order Import tool (pre-orders/crowdfunding); retail distribution "if it's an eligible product" (page header claims "over 40,000 book retailers", and selling "through online retailers like Amazon"). — Documented (eligibility criteria not stated)
9. Production time: 3–5 business days at all printers; day 1 is the day after ordering (example: Monday order prints Thursday–following Monday). — Documented
10. Free design bundle per size: page and cover templates for InDesign, Photoshop, and Word plus the Book Creation Guide (S20); Pricing Calculator gives print cost and custom cover template for exact page count. — Documented
11. Inconsistency: format cards default to Premium B&W / Standard B&W ink, while the FAQ recommends Standard Color ink; the S14 blog says uncoated is "a necessity" while this FAQ also recommends 80# coated. The FAQ also says "sell your magazine" (copy-paste slip). — Inferred
