## Learned points
1. Purpose: the tool gives exact cover dimensions and lets you download a template (PDF and PNG) to use as a guide layer in image-editing software. Documented
2. Inputs: Binding type (Hardcover / Paperback); Cover type (Hardcover, Dust Jacket); Interior type (Black & white, Premium color, Standard color); Paper type (White, Cream, Groundwood; the HTML also contains a "COLOR" paper value); Reading direction (Left to Right / Right to Left); Units (inches / millimeters); trim size; page count. Documented
3. Trim size presets in the static page: 5.5 x 8.5 in and 6 x 9 in, plus "Custom trim size" (other presets may load dynamically; not seen). Documented (static text only)
4. Custom trim limits shown: width 4-8.5 inches (101.6-215.9 mm); height 6-11.69 inches (152.4-296.92 mm). Documented
5. Page count is "Number of pages at your formatted trim size"; two validation ranges appear: 24-830 and 76-550 pages. The page does not say which binding each applies to (probably paperback vs hardcover). Documented (ranges) / Inferred (mapping)
6. Spine width and full-cover dimensions depend on binding, paper, interior type and page count, so a coloring-book cover must be sized after the final page count and paper are fixed. Inferred
7. "Groundwood paper" appears as a paper option; its availability and use for coloring books are not explained here. Documented (existence) / open question
