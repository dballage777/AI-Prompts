## Learned points
1. KDP prints on demand and deducts printing cost from royalties; no upfront cost or inventory. Amazon.com.br, .com.in and .com.mx do not support paperback/hardcover distribution. — Documented
2. Formula: Fixed cost + (page count * per page cost) = printing cost. Cost depends on trim size (regular vs large), page count, ink, paper type and the marketplace of purchase. Bleed and cover finish do NOT affect cost. — Documented
3. Black ink, cream/white paper, 24–110 pages (24–108 for .com.au): fixed cost only. 10. KDP's worked examples: 300-page B/W regular on .com = 1.00 + 300 x 0.012 = 4.60 USD; 55-page B/W regular on .co.uk = 1.93 GBP; 150-page standard color regular on .de = 4.35 EUR; 600-page standard color regular on .ca = 23.46 CAD; 50-page premium color large on .com.au = 7.42 AUD. — Documented
11. Coloring-book examples (8.5" x 11" is a large trim): 100-page B/W white -> 2.84 USD (fixed only, under 110 pages); 120-page B/W -> 1.00 + 120 x 0.017 = 3.04 USD; 100-page groundwood -> 2.75 USD; 100-page premium color -> 1.00 + 100 x 0.08 = 9.00 USD. — Inferred (arithmetic from the .com tables)
12. Minimum list price = printing cost / royalty rate (60% or 50% depending on list price band, see S8). KDP blocks list prices below the minimum. Example: 2.84 USD cost at the 50% band -> 5.68 USD minimum. — Documented (rule) / Inferred (example)
13. Maximum paperback list price: 250 USD; 350 CAD; 250 GBP; 250 EUR; 1,200 PLN; 2,500 SEK; 350 AUD; 30,000 YEN. Prices can be set per marketplace or auto-derived from the primary marketplace; manually set prices are not updated by later primary-price changes. — Documented
14. Page-band quirks: B/W bands are listed as "24-110" and "110-828" (110 appears in both); groundwood is 24–112 then 114–828; premium color 24–40 then 42–828 (page counts are even, so the gaps are consistent). — Documented (observed)

## Limitations

- Hardcover costs are on a separate page (not read). Taxes/VAT not covered here.
