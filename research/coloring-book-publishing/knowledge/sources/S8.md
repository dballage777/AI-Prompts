## Learned points
1. A royalty is earned when a paperback is bought via Amazon or Expanded Distribution and a new copy is manufactured. Japanese-language paperbacks cannot be enrolled in Expanded Distribution. — Documented
2. Amazon marketplaces: royalty rate is 50% or 60% depending on list price. Formula: (Royalty rate x list price) – printing costs = royalty. — Documented
3. Rate thresholds (50% at or below / 60% at or above): Amazon.com 9.98 / 9.99 USD; .de, .fr, .it, .es, .nl, .ie, .com.be 9.98 / 9.99 EUR; .co.uk 7.98 / 7.99 GBP; .ca 13.98 / 13.99 CAD; .com.au 13.98 / 13.99 AUD; .co.jp 999 / 1000 JPY; .pl 39 / 40 PLN; .se 109 / 110 SEK. — Documented
4. KDP's example: $15 list, 333-page regular-trim black ink, US: (0.60 x $15) – $5.00 = $4.00. — Documented
5. Expanded Distribution: 40% of list price (as effective in the channel at time of purchase) minus printing costs. Example: (0.40 x $15) – $5.00 = $1.00. Expanded Distribution royalties appear about 30 days after the end of the month of sale. — Documented
6. Coloring-book examples (.com, 8.5x11 B/W, 100 pages, printing 2.84 USD per S7): at $9.99 -> 0.60 x 9.99 – 2.84 = 3.15 USD; at $7.99 (50% band) -> 0.50 x 7.99 – 2.84 = 1.16 USD; Expanded Distribution at $9.99 -> 0.40 x 9.99 – 2.84 = 1.16 USD. Pricing just below 9.99 USD drops the rate to 50%. — Inferred (arithmetic)
7. List price = the price entered on KDP's pricing page; in some marketplaces Amazon adds VAT/GST/consumption tax on top, so customers see a tax-inclusive price. — Documented
8. A minimum list price ensures royalties cover printing; maximum list prices: 250 USD, 350 CAD, 250 EUR, 250 GBP, 350 AUD, 30,000 JPY, 1,200 PLN, 2,500 SEK. — Documented
9. Amazon promotional discounts below list price do not reduce your royalty. — Documented
10. Estimated royalty shows in the Rights & Pricing tab; royalties appear in reports as copies ship. — Documented
11. Contradiction: this page says "Trim size, bleed settings, and cover finish don't affect printing cost," but S7's tables charge more for large trims. S7 is the detailed source; treat this line as an error or outdated. — Documented (observed) / Inferred (resolution)
