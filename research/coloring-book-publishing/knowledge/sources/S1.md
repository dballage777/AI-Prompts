- Access: OPENED | Date read: 2026-11-09 | Published/updated: no date shown on page (footer "© 1996-2026"; footer stat "Total KDP Select Author Earnings September 2026", so page served current as of Nov 2026)
- Pages read: Content Guidelines (main); same-site subpage "Publishing Public Domain Content" https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200743940 (linked from the Public domain section). Not read: "Guide to Kindle Content Quality" (G200952510).
- Source type & possible bias: Official Amazon KDP policy page (primary). Amazon's own rules; wording is broad and gives Amazon discretion ("we reserve the right").
## Learned points
1. Guidelines apply to the book content, including title, cover art, and product description. Documented
2. Enforcement uses "machine learning, automation, and dedicated teams of human reviewers"; KDP will reject or remove non-compliant content, may request additional information before sale, and the author can appeal a removal. Documented
3. The author/publisher is responsible for ensuring content doesn't violate copyright, trademark, brand, privacy, publicity, or other rights. Documented
4. KDP will not accept copyrighted content that is freely available on the web unless provided by the copyright owner. Documented
5. AI disclosure is REQUIRED for AI-generated content (text, images, or translations) when publishing a new book or editing and republishing an existing one; AI-generated images explicitly include cover and interior images and artwork. Documented
6. Definition: content created by an AI-based tool is "AI-generated" even if you applied substantial edits afterwards. For a coloring book, AI-drawn line art = AI-generated and must be disclosed even if cleaned up by hand. Documented (application to coloring books: Inferred)
7. Definition: "AI-assisted" = you created the content yourself and used AI to edit, refine, error-check or improve it, or used AI only to brainstorm ideas; AI-assisted content need NOT be disclosed. Documented
8. You remain responsible for verifying that both AI-generated and AI-assisted content meets all guidelines, including intellectual property rights. Documented
9. Prohibited/offensive content: hate speech, child abuse/sexual exploitation, pornography, glorifying rape or pedophilia, terrorism, "or other material we deem inappropriate or offensive." Documented
10. Poor customer experience: no descriptive content that misleads or doesn't accurately represent the book; no content "typically disappointing to customers" (per Guide to Kindle Content Quality); Amazon may change a book's categories at any time. Documented
11. Public domain content is allowed, but undifferentiated versions are refused if a free version is in the store; KDP may ask for proof of public-domain status. Documented
12. (Subpage) A differentiated public-domain work must be Translated, Annotated, or Illustrated; "Illustrated" means 10 or more original illustrations relevant to the book; the title field must include "(Translated)", "(Annotated)" or "(Illustrated)". Documented
13. (Subpage) The product description must begin with a bullet-point summary of how the book is original (maximum 80 characters). Non-differentiating features include linked TOC, formatting improvements, collections, price, freely available internet content. Documented
14. (Subpage) Publishing rights choice at setup: "I own the copyright…" vs "This is a public domain work"; copyright duration varies by country; public-domain content is not eligible for all eBook royalty options or KDP Select. Documented
15. The public-domain differentiation rules are written around text books (e.g. Pride and Prejudice); how they apply to a coloring book built from public-domain artwork is not stated. Inferred
16. Translations must credit translator and original author in the contributor field ("Anonymous" if unknown). Documented
17. Prompt rule: any assistant workflow that generates coloring-page images with AI should tell the user the book must be flagged as AI-generated in KDP, and that IP checks remain the user's job. Inferred
## Exact quotes
- "If you used an AI-based tool to create the actual content (whether text, images, or translations), it is considered "AI-generated," even if you applied substantial edits afterwards." — https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200672390
- "AI-generated images include cover and interior images and artwork. You are not required to disclose AI-assisted content." — https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200672390
- "We will not accept content under copyright that is freely available on the web unless it's provided by the owner of the copyright." — https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200672390
- "Illustrated : 10 or more original illustrations relevant to the book" — https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200743940
