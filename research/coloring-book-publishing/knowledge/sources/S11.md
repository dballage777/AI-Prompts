## Learned points
1. Everything listed on Etsy must be "made, designed, handpicked or sourced by you" (the seller), and all items must also follow the Prohibited Items Policy. Listing images must meet the Listing Image Requirements. — Documented
2. The seller is responsible for making sure the shop and its items comply with Etsy's policies. — Documented
3. Reselling mass-produced items is not allowed. Reselling is not allowed in the Handmade category, where reselling means listing an item as handmade when the seller "weren't involved in designing or making that item." — Documented
4. A coloring book or page fits the "Designed by a seller" category. In that category a seller can offer an original design as a digital download, or use a third party to produce or print the design onto a physical item. — Documented (fit to coloring books is Inferred)
5. Digital items must be made and/or designed by the seller. They can be premade files or custom files delivered after purchase. — Documented
6. "Seller-prompted AI creations" belong in the "Designed by a seller" category and must disclose the use of AI. — Documented
7. If you use a production partner (for example, a printer for a physical coloring book), you must disclose that partner in the relevant listings. — Documented
8. Etsy allows AI tools "as long as they contribute their own creative input and follow Etsy's policies." The AI disclosure must be in the listing description. — Documented
9. Permitted AI examples given: a fantasy scene based on a seller's original prompt made with an AI generator, and a custom AI portrait of a buyer's pet. — Documented
10. Not allowed: bot-generated content; AI-generated functional instructions (craft patterns, recipes, instructions); AI prompt bundles. — Documented
11. Implication for coloring books: AI-assisted coloring pages appear permitted if the seller supplies original prompts or creative input and discloses AI in the description. Selling the prompts themselves as a bundle is not allowed. AI-written instructional text (for example, "how to color" technique guides) may fall under the ban on AI-generated functional instructions. — Inferred
12. Etsy does not give legal advice or decide whether shop content infringes someone's intellectual property. It suggests contacting the IP owner or an attorney. On "proper notice" of infringement, Etsy removes the material. — Documented
13. Prohibited categories include pornography and mature content, hate items, and violent items that glorify violence. These limit coloring-book themes (for example, adult coloring books with explicit content). — Documented (application Inferred)
14. Services in general, including rentals, are not allowed. Only services that produce a new tangible item that meets Seller Policies may be sold. — Documented
15. Anyone can report a listing that appears to break the policies. Etsy may review and/or remove such listings. — Documented
16. The help page does not define "creative input," how much AI is acceptable, or the exact wording of the disclosure. Those details would be in the Creativity Standards and the AI page, which were blocked. — Inferred
