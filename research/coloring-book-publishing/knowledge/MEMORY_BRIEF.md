# Memory Brief: Creating and Selling a Coloring Book

## Most important learned points
1. **Coloring books aren't "low-content" on KDP.** Don't tick that box. They need an ISBN (KDP's is free), and they can use Expanded Distribution [S3, S13, S28]. (Strong)
2. **AI art must be disclosed.**
   - KDP: AI-generated interior or cover images must be declared, "even if you applied substantial edits." AI-assisted work (editing or brainstorming only) doesn't need disclosure [S1].
   - Etsy: AI use goes in the listing description, and the seller must add creative input. AI prompt bundles and AI-written instructions are banned [S11].
   - (Strong)
3. **Prompt-only AI pages can't be copyrighted.** "Prompts do not alone provide sufficient control." Re-rolling or picking one output doesn't count [S15, S16]. What can be protected:
   - your own drawing, if it's still visible in the AI output;
   - substantial human edits;
   - creative selection and arrangement of the pages. In the Zarya of the Dawn case, the AI images themselves were excluded [S10, S16].
   A copyright registration must exclude the AI material [S15]. (Strong)
4. **Public domain needs proof.**
   - US works published before 1931 are public domain [S22]. LOC's rule of thumb: publication year + 100 − 4 [S19].
   - "No known restrictions" isn't the same as public domain, and a download button isn't clearance [S19].
   - Check both labels: the original artwork and the scan or digital copy [S18].
   - Being public domain in the US doesn't make it public domain abroad [S18, S19].
   - KDP: an "(Illustrated)" public-domain edition needs 10 or more original illustrations [S1].
   - (Strong)
5. **KDP specs for 8.5×11:**
   - It counts as a "large" trim [S2].
   - Bleed: 0.125 in on the top, bottom, and outside edges. The PDF page is 8.625×11.25 in [S2].
   - Margins: 0.375 in outside (with bleed); gutter 0.375 in for 24–150 pages [S2].
   - Pages: 24–590 [S2, S4].
   - (Documented)
6. **File rules:**
   - Interior: one PDF of single pages, 300 DPI minimum (600 maximum), flattened, fonts embedded, no crop marks. Lines at least 0.75 pt [S4].
   - Cover: one PDF with back, spine, and front [S9].
   - Spine text only at 80 or more pages [S9, S20].
   - (Strong)
7. **Cost and royalty on KDP:**
   - Printing an 8.5×11 black-ink book with 24–110 pages costs $2.84 [S7].
   - Royalty is 60% at $9.99 or more, 50% at $9.98 or less, and 40% through Expanded Distribution, minus printing [S8].
   - At $9.99 you earn about $3.15 per copy; at $7.99, about $1.16 (Inferred).
8. **Lulu:**
   - Bleed on all sides; 0.5 in safety margin; paperback minimum 32 pages; spine width = pages/444 + 0.06 in [S20, S24].
   - Coil binding lies flat [S30]. Starting price for 8.5×11 is $4.28 [S30].
9. **Design for coloring:**
   - Consistent, bold lines with open space. Simple and thick for kids; intricate for adults [S14, S17].
   - Print one side only to stop bleed-through; it doubles the page count [S14, S30].
   - Draw line art at 600 PPI with slightly aliased brushes [S23].
   - Most books have 16–50 illustrations [S17, S27].
10. **Proof and color-test** before selling [S6, S14, S20, S27].
11. **Metadata:**
    - Title plus subtitle under 200 characters. No keyword stuffing, trademarks, or "bestselling"/"free" [S13].
    - No URLs or reviews in the description. Up to 3 categories. Set a reading age for kids' books [S13].
    - Cover text must match the metadata, and the cover can't copy another book's look [S9, S13].
12. **Trademarks:** search the USPTO for exact and similar wording on related goods. Marks don't have to be identical to conflict [S21].
13. **Etsy digital listings:**
    - Up to 5 files of 20 MB each. File names (70 characters max) can't be edited later [S12].
    - Etsy's print-from-digital test is on by default for US and Canadian shops. Opting out is shop-wide [S25].
14. **Tools:** Krita (free) has no PDF export; build the PDF in Scribus [S23].
15. **Market:** don't mass-produce. Research the niche (Amazon autosuggest, sales ranks) and focus on quality [S17, S28, S29].

## Dated platform snapshot (Nov 2026)
- **KDP:** free ISBN; 24-page minimum; $2.84 print cost; 60/50/40% royalty; AI disclosure required.
- **Lulu:** 32-page paperback minimum; coil-bound option; prints in 3–5 business days.
- **Etsy:** digital downloads; must be made or designed by you; AI disclosed in the description.

## Prompt-writing rules for this topic
1. Ask first: audience, theme, platform, trim size, art source, and page count.
2. Check the rights for every page: record the source and URL, avoid trademarked characters and living artists' styles, and search the USPTO for the title.
3. Build in AI disclosure (KDP's question and the Etsy description). Explain the copyright limits and how human redrawing helps.
4. Include the exact platform specs. Never mix KDP and Lulu specs.
5. For AI line-art image prompts: black-and-white outlines only, no shading or gray fills, closed shapes, consistent weight, open space suited to the audience, and 300–600 PPI at final size. Clean up by hand.
6. Ask whether pages are single- or double-sided, and explain the trade-off.
7. Follow the metadata rules, and don't tick "low-content."
8. Show the royalty math and flag the $9.99 threshold.
9. Always end with: proof copy plus a color test.
10. Date the specs and tell users to verify them. Label evidence and disagreements.
