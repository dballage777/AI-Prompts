## Relevance to these two dogs (Inferred, 1–3 bullets)
- Dog 1 (55 lb): ~2 cups/day of this recipe ≈ 680 kcal/day by label arithmetic (2 × 340), which is already below the label's 51–100 lb range of 3 1/4–5 1/2 cups; the label table is not a weight-loss guide, so a vet-set calorie target matters more than the chart.
- Dog 2 (6-month puppy): the statement is "adult maintenance" only, so this food is not labeled for growth; it does not meet the AAFCO-label match for a growing puppy (see S18 for the brand's growth/all-life-stages option, S21 on life stages).
