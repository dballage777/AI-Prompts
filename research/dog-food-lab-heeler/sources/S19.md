# S19 — WSAVA Global Nutrition Guidelines (web page / toolkit index)
- URL: https://wsava.org/global-guidelines/global-nutrition-guidelines/ | Final URL: same
- Access: OPENED (static page text; it is mainly an index of linked PDFs/tools — linked tools other than S20 were not opened) | Date read: 2026-11-08 | Published/updated: page undated; guidelines launched 2011; calorie charts "Updated July 2020"
- Source type & bias: Expert body — World Small Animal Veterinary Association, Global Nutrition Committee. Aimed at veterinary teams; endorsed by many vet associations (incl. American Academy of Veterinary Nutrition, AAHA, BSAVA, ECVCN). Low commercial bias on this page.

## Findings (5–15; each: text [Q#] [label])
1. Purpose: Guidelines "aim to help veterinary teams ensure that pets are fed according to an optimal and individually tailored nutrition plan." [Q4][Q6] [Expert body]
2. Frames both malnutrition and obesity as problems: "in others, pet obesity is a growing problem." [Q4] [Expert body]
3. Offers body condition score (BCS) charts and video for dogs — described as tools to "accurately assess body condition, a measure of fat stores, at every visit." [Q4][Q6] [Expert body]
4. Muscle condition score charts: muscle "can be affected by disease or aging" — relevant to senior dogs, distinct from fat stores. [Q4] [Expert body]
5. Provides "Calorie Needs for Healthy Adult Dogs" — "starting points for calorie needs" (Updated July 2020). Not opened; no numbers recorded. [Q4] [Expert body]
6. Ingredient list caution: owners treat it as most important, but "the ingredient list gives no information on the quality of the ingredients and can be very misleading on the overall quality of the food." [Q6] [Expert body]
7. Links "Selecting a Pet Food" tool (S20) and North America/Europe label guides for owners. [Q6] [Expert body]
8. Hosts a "Raw Meat Based Diets for Pets" infographic that "sums up the potential risks" (not opened). [Q2] [Expert body]
9. Also offers a Short Diet History Form and Nutritional Assessment Checklist for nutrition screening "on every patient at every visit." [Q4][Q7] [Expert body]
