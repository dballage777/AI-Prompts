## Learned points
1. Federal job applications start at USAJOBS.gov. The user must sign in to USAJOBS to apply. — [Documented]
2. Before applying, complete the required sections of your USAJOBS profile and keep other profile information up to date. Some of this information is sent to the hiring agency during the application. — [Documented]
3. Find a job announcement by keyword or location, or with Saved Searches. — [Documented]
4. Review the announcement, especially the "How to Apply" and "Required Documents" sections, before clicking Apply. — [Documented]
5. The USAJOBS apply flow has five steps: Select resume → Select documents → Review package → Include personal info → Continue application with agency. — [Documented]
6. In the final step the applicant certifies the application is "true, complete, and made in good faith." Checking the box and clicking "Continue to agency site" hands off to the agency. "Previous" lets them go back and edit. — [Documented]
7. The application is finished on the hiring agency's own system after the USAJOBS handoff. A bot should tell users that being redirected from USAJOBS to an agency site is normal, and that the start point should still be a real USAJOBS.gov announcement. — [Documented; advice Inferred]
8. OPM links USAJOBS Help Center articles on editing, continuing, and saving an application, and on how the process works. Those articles could not be read here (403), so nothing about their content is recorded. — [Documented existence; content not read]
9. Nothing on this page asks applicants for payment. The flow is sign-in, profile, documents, certification, then the agency. A request for a fee to apply for a federal job does not appear anywhere in this official process. — [Inferred]
