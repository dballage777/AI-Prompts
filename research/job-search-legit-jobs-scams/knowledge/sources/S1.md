## Learned points
1. Scam category 1: work-from-home offers promising high pay for little work, including reshipping packages, reselling luxury goods, and paying for starter kits, training, or certifications. — [Documented] (summary-only)
2. Scam category 2: fake check scams. The "employer" sends a check, you deposit it, keep part, and send the rest on. The check bounces and the bank wants the full amount repaid. — [Documented]
3. Scam category 3: mystery shopping "jobs" that require paying for certifications, job directories, or guarantees. — [Documented] (summary-only)
4. Scam category 4: job placement firms that charge candidates a fee, especially upfront. Honest placement firms are typically paid by the hiring company, not the candidate. — [Documented]
5. Scam category 5: ads for government or postal jobs that charge a fee. Applying for federal or postal jobs is always free. — [Documented]
6. Verification step: search the company or hiring person's name with words like "scam," "review," or "complaint." — [Documented] (summary-only)
7. Talk the offer over with someone you trust before acting. — [Documented] (summary-only)
8. Core rule: don't pay for the promise of a job. — [Documented]
9. Don't trust a "cleared" check. Honest employers won't ask you to send money back or to buy gift cards with it. — [Documented] (summary-only)
10. Safe places to look, named by the FTC: USAJobs.gov (federal), CareerOneStop (US Department of Labor), and USA.gov for state and local government job listings. — [Documented] (summary-only)
11. If you paid a scammer: contact the payment company right away, report the fraud, and ask for a reversal. Methods covered are debit or credit card, mobile payment app, wire transfer, gift card, cash reload card, and cryptocurrency. The page points to the FTC's "What To Do If You Were Scammed" article. — [Documented] (summary-only)
12. Reporting channels: ReportFraud.ftc.gov, your state attorney general (directory at naag.org), and more scam information at ftc.gov/scams. — [Documented]
13. Bot implication: the bot should point users to the official boards above, treat any request for payment to apply or get hired as a scam signal, and give these reporting steps when a user has already paid. — [Inferred]
