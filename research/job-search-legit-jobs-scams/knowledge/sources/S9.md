## Learned points
1. Scope: applies to any "API Consumer" (individual or organization that registers for or uses credentials); OPM administers via USAJOBS Program Office; OPM may update terms and notify by email or the service; continued use = acceptance unless affirmative acceptance required; contact access@usajobs.gov. — Documented
2. Credentials: must provide current and accurate registration and contact info; consumer is responsible for confidentiality of credentials and all activity under them; must promptly notify access@usajobs.gov of suspected unauthorized use or security incidents. — Documented
3. Commercial use and redistribution: Public Data (Search, Historic JOA, Announcement Text, public code lists) is under an open license (44 U.S.C. §§ 3502, 3506(b)(6)); may be copied, stored, reformatted, adapted, analyzed, published, redistributed, and used for lawful commercial or noncommercial purposes; prior OPM approval not required. — Documented
4. Credentials govern access to the API, not lawful downstream reuse; data flagged in metadata/notice as under specific legal or third-party IP restriction is excluded from Public Data. — Documented
5. Attribution is encouraged, not required: identify USAJOBS as source, link to the current official announcement, include retrieval date, and disclose that an unofficial product is not operated, sponsored, approved, or endorsed by OPM. — Documented
6. Accuracy/non-affiliation prohibitions: may modify, summarize, analyze, or combine data but may not falsely present modified info as official/unaltered; falsely claim OPM or other federal agency endorsement; misuse seals/logos or agency names implying affiliation; falsely represent that an application was transmitted to or received by USAJOBS or an agency; or engage in phishing, fraud, or deceptive collection of applicant information. — Documented
7. Applications must be submitted according to the official instructions in the job announcement. — Documented
8. Content: public announcements may include agency point-of-contact names and business contacts; API does not provide applicant profiles, applications, account info, or other nonpublic user data; not authorized for classified info or CUI. — Documented
9. Conduct prohibitions: violate privacy/publicity rights; share or transfer credentials; circumvent authentication, rate limits, or safeguards; access nonpublic data; disrupt or unreasonably burden systems; impersonate; transmit malicious code; use in phishing/fraud. — Documented
10. Privacy: OPM collects registration and account-activity data, handles it under the Privacy Act and SORN, and may monitor API activity for security and misuse. — Documented
11. Storage and rate limits: OPM provides no archival storage; OPM may set/revise rate limits, transaction quotas, response-size limits; current limits are in developer docs; OPM may impose immediate limits without advance notice for security, misuse, or availability. — Documented
12. Termination: consumer may stop and request deactivation anytime; OPM may suspend/revoke for violations, suspected fraud, unreasonable burden, inactivity, or service changes; revocation does not bar lawful reuse of data already obtained. — Documented
13. Links: OPM is not responsible for external sites in Public Data and does not endorse by linking. — Documented
14. Liability/warranty: no liability for damages to the extent permitted by law; sovereign immunity not waived; service and data "as is" and "as available"; no warranty that data is complete, current, or error-free; consumers must verify against the official announcement. — Documented
15. No third-party beneficiaries; entire agreement for the API; non-waiver, severability; governed by federal law. — Documented
16. Bot implication: the bot may show and summarize USAJOBS data, should say it is not affiliated with or endorsed by OPM, should link to the official announcement with retrieval date, must never say it "applied" or "submitted" for the user, and must tell users to apply via the official instructions and to verify status/close dates there. — Inferred
