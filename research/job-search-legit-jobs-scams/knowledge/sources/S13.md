## Learned points
1. Scale: in the last three years, nearly 50,000 people reported to BBB Scam Tracker that they had fallen victim to an employment scam. Reports doubled in 2025 compared with 2024. — [Research]
2. Underreporting: BBB cites a study using FTC data that found only 4.8% of mass-market fraud victims report it to BBB or a government entity. Real numbers are likely much higher. — [Research]
3. The dominant new type is "task-based scams." Targets are paid to like, follow, or subscribe to videos, or to rate and review products or apps ("app optimization," "product boosting"). These scams became pervasive in 2025 via SMS, WhatsApp, and other text-based messages. — [Research]
4. Task-scam mechanics: an unsolicited text leads to a move to WhatsApp or Telegram, then sometimes a fake interview, then an immediate "hire." The target works on a gamified dashboard on a site or app that impersonates a real business, with "levels" (e.g., 40 tasks). A small early payout builds trust. Withdrawing then requires depositing the target's own money, often cryptocurrency, to "unlock" earnings or clear a negative balance. — [Reported]
5. Numbers: nearly 680 of the 2025 employment-scam reports to BBB appear to be task scams. BBB's median task-scam loss was $2,300 in 2025. BBB cites the FTC as seeing a 400% increase in task scams in 2024. — [Research]
6. Victim losses described (first names only): $65,000, $57,000, $100,000, and $140,000. One victim expects 10 years to repay debts. BBB attributes the escalation to sunk-cost pressure. — [Reported]
7. Channel: half of the over 23,000 employment-scam reports in 2025 listed text messages as the delivery method. The texts often impersonated recruiting firms, YouTube, or major studios (Warner Bros. Discovery, Disney, Universal Pictures). — [Research]
8. Consumer Reports, cited by BBB, found text scams rose 50% in 2025. People aged 19–29 and Black and Latino consumers are at higher risk of reporting losses. — [Research]
9. ITRC's COO, quoted by BBB, says AI makes fake websites and apps easier to build. Remote work and layoffs make job scams lucrative, and remote work is new to many people, so it "is not setting off any alarms." — [Reported]
10. Repeated scammer personas ("Goldie," "Rebecca Harris") and identical texts across reports suggest common scam groups. — [Reported]
11. BBB red flags: unprompted job offers, too-good-to-be-true salaries, high-pressure offers to take the job immediately, interviewers who refuse to turn on the camera, payment to like, follow, or subscribe to videos or rate an app, charges or taxes to withdraw money already earned, and upfront costs to begin work. — [Documented]
12. BBB tips: research companies offering jobs, use video in interviews when possible, never pay money to get your paycheck, avoid jobs that pay you to watch, like, follow, or subscribe, and contact authorities if you believe a job owes you money. — [Documented]
13. Where to report: BBB or BBB Scam Tracker, the FTC (or 877-FTC-Help), and for Canada the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre (1-888-495-8501). BBB also offers a Scam Survivor Toolkit. — [Documented]
14. Emotional harm: almost 50% of people targeted by a scam reported losing confidence or peace of mind (BBB research). The page tells victims "you are not alone." — [Research]
15. For the bot: any "job" that pays for likes, reviews, or app ratings, or requires a deposit to withdraw earnings, should be called a scam. A user who has lost money should get reporting steps plus emotional support, without shame. — [Inferred]

## Limitations / bias
- Self-reported Scam Tracker data, which is not representative and is undercounted (4.8% reporting rate). The task-scam count (~680) is a BBB classification estimate.
- Charts (Scam Tracker 2023–2025, Consumer Sentinel) are images and were not read. FTC and Consumer Reports numbers are second-hand via BBB.
- No publication date is shown. Victim anecdotes are illustrative, not statistical.
## Best for
- Current data and plain red-flag and tip lists on task-based or text-message job scams, plus a reminder to address victims' emotional harm.
