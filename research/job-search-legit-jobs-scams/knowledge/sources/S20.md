## Learned points
1. NLx describes itself as "the only nonprofit national online labor exchange system in the United States." It collects job openings only from corporate career websites, state job banks, and federal government job sites. — [Documented]
2. Figures shown on the site (undated): "Current Employer Count: 300,000"; "Daily Job Count: 3 million." — [Documented]
3. Quality claims: jobs are collected from authentic sources ("no duplicates or scams") and refreshed daily ("no dead links"). Wherever a seeker finds an NLx job, they are returned to the point of origin, either the employer's career site or the state job bank. — [Documented]
4. Participation: all 50 states, DC, Puerto Rico, the U.S. Virgin Islands, and Guam take part. Federal agencies, nonprofits, diversity and disability partners, and Veteran Service Organizations also participate. — [Documented]
5. History: America's Job Bank (federally funded, begun 1995) was replaced by the NLx in 2007. DOL fully discontinued AJB on June 30, 2007. In June 2012, 20,000 USAJOBS federal jobs were added. By July 2016, all states and territories were exchanging job data. — [Documented]
6. The State Job Banks page links each state or territory's workforce agency job site. In-state employers can post jobs free there, and registration, validation, and posting times vary by state. This list is a ready directory of official state job banks. — [Documented; last sentence Inferred]
7. Search: the home form takes "job title or keywords" and "city, state, country" and submits to /jobs/. The URL /jobs/?q=<keywords>&location=<place> loads, but results render client-side from a DirectEmployers search API (prod-search-api.jobsyn.org). No public API terms were found on usnlx.com. — [Inferred from page form and site scripts]
8. Apply: the site's "Apply Now" button routes through a DirectEmployers redirect domain (rr.jobsyn.org / nlx.jobsyn.org) to the original posting, and some jobs use a mailto. Expired jobs show a notice: "If you found this page from a job link, then that job is expired." — [Inferred from site scripts; quote Documented]
9. Posting rules (Terms 10.2) ban: commission-only jobs; jobs paying under minimum wage; jobs requiring the applicant to make a capital investment or attend unpaid training; MLM, franchise, distributorship, or "club membership" solicitations; chain letters or pyramid schemes; false or misleading info; and "applicant harvesting." These map directly to job-scam red flags. — [Documented]
10. "Applicant Harvesting" is defined as using "false, fictitious, misleading or alluring job postings" to entice a job seeker to give personal or employment information they would not give if they knew the intent. — [Documented]
11. Postings must be "individual openings for traditional, W-2 or 1099 employees," must be detailed enough to show the job's nature and requirements, and may not just say "email for more details." — [Documented]
12. Moderation limits: the Association is "under no obligation to monitor the posted materials," may monitor at random, and makes no guarantees about accuracy. Reliance on user postings is "at your own risk." So even NLx listings deserve a quick check. — [Documented; last sentence Inferred]
13. Report inappropriate postings or conduct to info@usnlx.com. — [Documented]
14. Use licenses: job seekers get a limited right to use the site only for personal job seeking. Reproducing or distributing content for public or commercial purposes is barred. Probing or scanning systems and "overloading" are prohibited. An AI tool should not scrape USNLx; it should link users to it or seek authorized data access (see S17). — [Documented; last sentence Inferred]
15. The Terms are governed by Indiana law. The Association "is not an employment agency nor a recruiting firm" and does not guarantee hiring results. — [Documented]
16. Privacy: there is a resume database that member companies, and non-members for a nominal fee, can access. The site warns that no career site can assure a resume "will not be collected and shared with others." Users should leave sensitive data (SSN, DOB) off resumes. — [Documented; advice Inferred]
17. Special-population microsites: veterans.usnlx.com, ability.usnlx.com (disability), and virtualjobs.usnlx.com (remote). — [Documented]

## Limitations / bias
- Self-promotional figures (300,000 employers, 3 million daily jobs) are undated and unaudited.
- The "no scams" claim conflicts with the Terms' disclaimer of monitoring duty and accuracy, so treat it as aspirational.
- The Terms and Privacy Policy look old (references to .asp pages and NACElink), and there is no effective date.
- Live search behavior, filters, and result fields were not observed directly.
## Best for
- Giving the bot a nonprofit, state-linked national job search plus a state job bank directory, and a concrete list of posting types that legitimate boards ban (capital investment, unpaid training, MLM, applicant harvesting) to use as red flags.
