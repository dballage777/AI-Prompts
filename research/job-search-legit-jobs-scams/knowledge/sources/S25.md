## Learned points
1. Scam Tracker lets the public report scams and search reported ones. The search box takes a phone number, website, email, business name or scam ID. The lookup page also suggests natural-language queries, for example "romance scams in Texas over $5,000". — Documented
2. Lookup filters: Date Reported (from/to; the default window shown was the last month), Dollars Lost (from/to), Country (United States or Canada), State/Province, and Scam Type. The unfiltered default showed "10,000+" results. — Documented
3. Each published report shows the scam type and subtype (e.g., "Employment - Employment Scam"), a free-text narrative, Dollars Lost, victim location (city, state, ZIP), Date Reported and "Business Name Used". — Documented
4. How it works: Report, then Review, then Publish. BBB reviews each report before publishing it and never shows the reporter's name or contact details publicly. — Documented
5. The report form is a 9-step flow. Step 1 asks who was targeted (yourself, another person, or your business) and the type of experience. One listed option is "I was approached about a fake job offer". Reporters are asked to include phone numbers, emails, websites, payment methods and messages. — Documented
6. There is also a heatmap (US and Canada, last 90 days, filterable by region and scam type), a Scam Survival Kit, a Scam Prevention Guide, scam studies and scam tips. — Documented
7. Employment reports seen on 2026-11-13: an impersonation "interview" call where the caller already knew the person's private details; an MLM posing as legitimate hiring; and a remote "data entry" offer paid by check for equipment that ended in gift-card payments. In that last case the reporter said the scammer cited seeing their resume on CALjobs (California's state job bank). — Reported
8. The CALjobs report suggests scammers may harvest resumes posted on public or state job banks. This comes from a single report and is not verified. — Reported / Inferred
9. Accuracy caveat (Terms): BBB asks submitters to affirm that information is accurate but does not verify what users submit. Reports are user claims, not findings. — Documented
10. Terms: unless BBB gives written consent, you may use, download and copy BBB Content only for personal, non-commercial use. BBB Content is informational, its accuracy is not guaranteed, and relying on it is at your own risk. You may not use BBB trademarks without written consent. — Documented
11. Searching the Terms of Use text for "automat", "scrap", "robot", "crawl" and "harvest" found no explicit clause on automated access. The binding limit found is the personal, non-commercial rule plus the written-consent requirement. — Documented (search of retrieved text)
12. robots.txt applies the same rules to named AI agents (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Claude-User, Claude-SearchBot, anthropic-ai, PerplexityBot, CCBot and others) and to all other user agents. It sets `Content-signal: search=yes, ai-input=yes, ai-train=yes`. It disallows `/*?` (any URL with a query string, such as filtered searches) and some paths (complaint forms, business-reviews, authentication). — Documented
13. For an AI assistant: tell users to search Scam Tracker themselves for a recruiter's phone number, email, domain or company name, and to report there. Don't scrape filtered search results (blocked by robots.txt), and don't republish reports commercially without BBB consent. Present any hits as unverified user reports, and note that no hits does not mean an employer is safe. — Inferred
14. Published reports include victim city and ZIP and sometimes names inside the narratives. A bot should not copy or repeat these personal details. — Inferred

## Limitations / bias
- The "Review" step is not described in detail. It is unclear how BBB's pre-publication review fits with the Terms statement that it does not verify accuracy.
- Live counts (reports tracked, losses) on the home page are loaded by script and were not captured.
- Only step 1 of the report form was visible without interaction. Scam tips and studies subpages were not opened.
- No public API documentation was found.
## Best for
- Telling users how to cross-check a recruiter's phone, email or domain against public scam reports and how to report a job scam, with the right caveats (reports are unverified; personal and non-commercial use only).
