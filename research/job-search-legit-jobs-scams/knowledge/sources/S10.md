## Learned points
1. American Job Centers (AJCs), also called one-stop centers, are the local delivery points of the public workforce system; Wagner-Peyser Employment Service offices are collocated with them under WIOA. — Documented (summary-only)
2. Count differs by page: main AJC page says approximately 2,300 AJCs; Wagner-Peyser page says nearly 2,400; 2021 fact sheet says "almost 2,400 delivery points." — Documented
3. Fact sheet: DOL ETA funds AJCs through State Workforce Boards; centers are operated by community colleges, community-based organizations, and government agencies. — Documented
4. Job-seeker services: job search and placement help, job referrals, re-employment services for UI claimants, skills assessment, career guidance and pathways planning, local labor-market and wage info, training access, industry-recognized certifications, apprenticeship connections. — Documented
5. Delivery modes: self-service, facilitated self-help, and staff-assisted. — Documented (summary-only)
6. Wagner-Peyser goal: universal access for all job seekers and employers to basic labor exchange services; some services (e.g., WIOA-funded individualized services/training) have eligibility requirements; services vary by state. — Documented (summary-only)
7. WIOA Adult priority of service: public-assistance recipients, other low-income individuals, and those basic-skills deficient; veterans get priority of service in all DOL-funded employment programs and priority referrals to jobs and training. — Documented (summary-only)
8. Populations served: veterans, people with disabilities, migrant and seasonal farmworkers, justice-involved individuals, youth, older workers. — Documented (summary-only)
9. The pages read do not explicitly say services are free (the summaries noted no mention of fees); do not assert "free" from these pages alone. — Documented (absence)
10. How to find one: CareerOneStop AJC Finder (careeronestop.org/LocalHelp/AmericanJobCenters/find-american-job-centers.aspx); toll-free 1-877-US-2JOBS. TTY number differs by source: web pages list 1-877-872-5627 (summary-only); 2021 fact sheet lists 1-877-889-5627 (TTY) and the older www.servicelocator.org. — Documented
11. DOL Job Seekers page names CareerOneStop as the first source for career exploration, training, job search, and local help; also lists My Next Move, Apprenticeship.gov job finder, Job Corps (free residential training, ages 16–24), and CareerOneStop pages on job banks, job fairs, job clubs, resumes, and researching employers. — Documented (summary-only)
12. Unemployment insurance is run by each state; DOL routes people via CareerOneStop. — Documented
13. DOL Job Seekers page contains no specific job-scam warnings (only general .gov/https site-security notes). — Documented (summary-only)
14. Bot implication: recommend the local AJC (via CareerOneStop finder) for free-to-low-barrier human help (resume, training, job referrals), note that services and eligibility vary by state, and use the CareerOneStop finder link rather than hard-coding a TTY number. — Inferred
