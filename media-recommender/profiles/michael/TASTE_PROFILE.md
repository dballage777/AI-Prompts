# Michael: taste profile
Adult · created 2026-11-18 from Michael's S–F tier list · confirmed OK

**S:** Re:Zero, The Apothecary Diaries, Tokyo Ghoul, Chainsaw Man · **A:** Bleach TYBW, My Hero Academia, Goodnight Punpun, FMA: Brotherhood, Vinland Saga

**Dominant appeals:** (1) psychological depth: heroes pushed to the breaking point who change because of it (Re:Zero, Tokyo Ghoul, Punpun); (2) emotional bonds with real weight and loss; (3) action that means something (TYBW, MHA).

**Also loves:** theology (gods, faith, sin, afterlife); clever, character-driven mystery (Apothecary Diaries).

**Mood:** dark, heavy, emotional welcome. Intimate character journeys and redemption (Vinland, FMA:B, A) beat large-scale war spectacle (AOT, B).

**Lower:** thin-plot power fantasy (Solo Leveling, One-Punch Man C; Overlord, Slime B).

**Hard filters:** no fan service (Mushoku Tensei F); no mindless plots; skip seen titles.

**Popularity:** mixed, mainstream to cult. **Defaults:** solo; any length; mix it up.
