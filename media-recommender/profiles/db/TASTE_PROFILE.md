# DB: taste profile
Adult · reassessed 2026-11-18

**S:** Bleach, Bleach TYBW, Solo Leveling, Blue Lock, Dr. Stone, Mob Psycho 100 · **A+:** Demon Slayer, Black Clover, Kuroko's Basketball · **A:** JJK, Kaiju No. 8, Re:Zero, Gachiakuta

**Dominant appeals:** (1) a hero with a unique edge who wins by outthinking or outgrowing everyone (Dr. Stone, Blue Lock, Kuroko's, Solo Leveling, Mob); (2) sports played like battles (Blue Lock, Kuroko's); (3) cool, stylish sword/supernatural fights (Bleach, Demon Slayer, JJK).

**Mood:** hype and fun with heart. Gritty is fine when it's stylish and hype (Gachiakuta A); hopeless or bleak ranks lower (AOT B, Deadman C). Mecha ranks low.

**Hard filters:** no heavy-romance stories or simp leads; no shows that fall off in quality; no movies; no screaming-heavy shows or grating voices (Dragon Ball, Naruto); nothing slow (Frieren). Dated animation only if the cool factor is huge (Bleach).

**Defaults:** solo; any length; mainstream; mix it up.
