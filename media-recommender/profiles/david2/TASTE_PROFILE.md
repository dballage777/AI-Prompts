# David#2: taste profile
Adult · rebuilt 2026-11-17 · not the same person as David

**All-time #1:** Bleach: Thousand-Year Blood War (epic sword-and-power war, modern animation).

**Dominant appeals:** (1) a determined underdog who keeps growing stronger (Black Clover, Solo Leveling, Bleach, Seven Deadly Sins, Kaiju No. 8); (2) high stakes in a dark, dangerous world with monster or war fights (Attack on Titan, Deadman Wonderland, Jujutsu Kaisen, Gachiakuta, Demon Slayer).

**Secondary:** action-comedy (Mob Psycho 100, Sakamoto Days); clever problem-solving (Dr. Stone); game worlds and sports played like battles (Shangri-La Frontier, Blue Lock); squads and corps.

**Mood:** hype and intense; gore OK. Pacing must stay fast: no slow, quiet, reflective shows (Frieren was too boring).

**Hard filters:** no love-interest-driven stories; series only (no movies); no shows known for dropping off in quality; newer animation, roughly 2015+ (Attack on Titan is an exception); skip seen titles.

**Popularity:** mainstream. **Defaults:** solo; any length; mix it up.
