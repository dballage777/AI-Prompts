## 2. How to start each path

### Rank 1: Mercor education pool
1. Apply only through **work.mercor.com**, to the "Education & Training Expert" listing.
2. Have ready: your resume, about 20 minutes for an AI interview, and proof of your teaching experience.
3. Expect the gap before your first project to be "a week or several months." Mercor's own posting says so.
4. Projects can end without warning, and Mercor faces a misclassification lawsuit. Treat this as irregular income.
5. Mercor pays weekly through Stripe or Wise. It's 1099 income.

### Rank 2: IDOE item writing
1. Sign up for IDOE newsletters (GovDelivery) and watch the IDOE assessment pages for "item writer" calls.
2. Ask your district testing coordinator to forward IDOE writer and review calls to you.
3. Training days that fall on contract days may pay your school for a substitute, not you. Last time, the $300 honorarium applied only on non-contract days.
4. You'll sign an NDA, and IDOE owns what you write (summary-only). You can't reuse these items anywhere.

### Rank 3: Independent PD
1. Use `03_PD_Offer_AI_for_Busy_Teachers.md` as your one-page offer.
2. Start with one low-risk session: a virtual 60–90 minute webinar at $150–$300. That gets you a testimonial and an evaluation summary.
3. Then pitch half days to neighboring districts, charter networks and private schools. Avoid your own district unless the pay goes through payroll (conflict-of-interest law).
4. Keep a simple agreement covering date, fee, cancellation and recording rights. Have a W-9 ready.

### Rank 4: DataAnnotation
1. Apply at **dataannotation.tech**. The starter assessment takes about an hour and **can be taken only once**, so take it when you're rested.
2. DataAnnotation pays through PayPal a few days after you request it.
3. There's no ban notice and no appeals process (Anecdotal report), so quality matters on every task.

### Rank 5: Curriculum contracts
1. Search LinkedIn and EdSurge jobs for "curriculum writer contract," "instructional design contractor" and "educator consultant."
2. Lead with your differentiation work: tiered versions, checked answer keys and reading-level measurement.
3. Set a floor of about $35/hr. Pass on $20/hr postings.

### Rank 6: Service-center PD
1. Email Central Indiana ESC (Keep Indiana Learning) and your regional ESC with the pitch in file 03.
2. Ask: "How do you contract outside presenters, and what is your presenter fee?" Their price to districts is $1,540–$2,200/day; your fee will be a share of that.

### Rank 7: General prompt engineering
- Skip this until summer. Then add one Contra or LinkedIn service, "Custom assistant setup for schools and nonprofits," using file 01.

## 3. Earnings scenarios (Inferred; not a promise)

These assume work is actually available. AI training projects come and go, and PD bookings take months to build.

| Scenario | School year (about 15 hrs/week) | Summer (about 10 weeks) | Gross for the year |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Low:** Mercor is slow, DataAnnotation fills in | 5 hrs/week × $25 for 30 weeks = $3,750 | 15 hrs/week × $25 = $3,750 | **about $7,500** |
| **Middle:** Mercor at $40, plus 2 PD half days | 8 hrs/week × $40 for 30 weeks = $9,600 | 25 hrs/week × $40 = $10,000, plus 2 × $500 PD = $1,000 | **about $20,600** |
| **High:** Mercor steady, an IDOE round, plus PD | 12 hrs/week × $45 for 30 weeks = $16,200 | 30 hrs/week × $45 = $13,500, plus 4 × $600 PD = $2,400, plus 25 items × $125 = $3,125 | **about $35,200** |

**Set aside 25–35% for taxes (Inferred).** That covers:
- 15.3% self-employment tax;
- Indiana state tax (2.95%) and county tax;
- federal income tax, which stacks on top of your teaching salary.

Make quarterly estimated payments, or raise the withholding on your school paycheck. Have a tax professional check the right amount.
