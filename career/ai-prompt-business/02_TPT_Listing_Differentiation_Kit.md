## Product contents
1. **Teacher guide** (4–6 pages): what the kit does, a 15-minute walkthrough, and tips for co-teaching teams.
2. **Three tiered prompts:**
   - **Reteach:** simpler language and worked examples.
   - **On level:** guided practice.
   - **Enrichment:** extension tasks.
   - Each prompt uses [VARIABLES] for the subject, standard and reading level.
3. **Answer-key check prompt:** asks the AI to solve every item and flag any mismatches.
4. **Sample outputs:** one made-up 9th-grade lesson run through all three tiers.
5. **Troubleshooting page:** what to do if the reading level is too high, the answers are wrong, or the AI drifts off the standard.
6. **One-page quick card**, printable.
