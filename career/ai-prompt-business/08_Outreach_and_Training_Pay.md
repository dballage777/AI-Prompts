**Reality check (Inferred):**
- National vendors charge thousands a day, but Indiana small businesses can get basic AI training for free.
- Don't sell "intro to ChatGPT." Sell **hands-on, done-with-you training on the business's own tasks**, often bundled with a custom assistant build.

## 2. Approaching school districts

**Who to contact**, in this order:
1. **Education service centers (ESCs):** Central Indiana ESC (Keep Indiana Learning) and your regional ESC. Ask: "How do you add outside presenters?"
2. **Curriculum or instruction directors and assistant superintendents** in nearby districts (not your own). They own the PD budgets.
3. **Technology or digital learning directors:** they approve the AI tools.
4. **Districts with IDOE Digital Learning or AI grants:** IDOE posts the recipients.

**Timing (Inferred; confirm locally):**
- Pitch summer PD in **January–March**.
- Pitch fall back-to-school PD in **March–May**.
- Pitch PD days during the year **6–8 weeks ahead**.

**Step-by-step**

| Step | What to do | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Clear your own district first: send the outside-activity notice and get ownership clearance (file 07, section 3). | Before any outreach |
| 2 | Build your sales kit: a one-page offer (file 03), a safety and privacy summary, a 5-minute demo video with made-up data, and a quote template (file 07). | Week 1 |
| 3 | Send personalized emails to 1 ESC and 5 districts (script A). | Week 2 |
| 4 | Follow up once after 7 days (script B). | Week 3 |
| 5 | Hold a 15-minute call (agenda below). | As booked |
| 6 | Send a written quote within 2 days. Offer a pilot. | After the call |
| 7 | Deliver. Collect an anonymous survey, ask for a written reference, and ask "Who else should I talk to?" | After the session |

**Script A: first email to a district**
```
Subject: Hands-on AI PD for secondary teachers (staff-only, no student data)

Hello [Name],

I'm a high school teacher in Indiana who builds and tests AI tools for
classroom use, such as a lesson differentiation assistant that checks its
own answer keys and a gradebook "who's behind" list builder.

I offer a 90-minute hands-on session, "AI for Busy Teachers," plus a
ready-to-use assistant kit that runs inside your district's own AI
accounts. It is staff-facing only, needs no student data, and follows
IDOE's AI guidance.

Would a 15-minute call in the next two weeks be useful? I've attached a
one-page overview.

Thank you,
[Name] | [personal email] | [phone] | [LinkedIn]
```

**Script B: follow-up**
```
Subject: Re: Hands-on AI PD for secondary teachers

Hello [Name], just following up on my note about AI PD for your
secondary staff. If spring or summer PD is still being planned, I'm
offering a pilot rate for the first two districts: [price] for one
building, including the session and the assistant kit. Happy to send a
short demo video. Thanks, [Name]
```

**15-minute call agenda**
1. Ask: "What takes your teachers the most time right now?" Listen and take notes.
2. Ask: "What AI tools and accounts does the district already have? Is there an AI policy?"
3. Show one 3-minute demo tied to their answer.
4. Offer the matching package and price (file 07).
5. Ask: "Who else needs to approve this, and what paperwork do you need from me?" (W-9, insurance, data agreement.)

**Common objections and answers (Inferred)**

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "We already have MagicSchool or another AI platform." | "Great. My session teaches the thinking behind good prompts and works inside the tools you already pay for." |
| "We're worried about student data." | "This is staff-only, and teachers are trained never to enter student data. Here's my one-page safety summary." |
| "No budget." | "The pilot costs less than a substitute-heavy PD day. Title II or digital learning grant funds may apply; your business office can confirm." |
| "We need board approval." | "Understood. I can send a quote and W-9 now so it's ready for your next meeting." |

## 3. Approaching businesses

**Who to contact (Inferred):**
- Small businesses with repetitive writing or admin work: real estate, insurance, dental and medical offices (no patient data), contractors, retailers, restaurants.
- Nonprofits and churches.
- Chambers of commerce, Rotary and Kiwanis clubs, and public libraries: places to get in front of groups.
- **Indiana SBDC offices:** they run AI workshops (summary-only). Offer to co-present or be a guest speaker.

**Step-by-step**

| Step | What to do | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Build one case study: a free or low-cost assistant for a friend's business or a nonprofit, with before and after (file 06). | Weeks 1–3 |
| 2 | Offer a free 30–45 minute talk at a chamber lunch, library or Rotary meeting: "3 AI Tasks That Save a Small Office 5 Hours a Week." | Month 1–2 |
| 3 | End the talk with a sign-up sheet for a free 20-minute "AI task check" call. | At the talk |
| 4 | On the call, find one task worth automating and quote a workshop or build. | Within a week |
| 5 | Deliver the work. Collect a testimonial and ask for referrals. | After the project |
| 6 | Post the case study (with permission) on LinkedIn and Contra. | Ongoing |

**Script C: email to a chamber or library**
```
Subject: Free member talk: "3 AI Tasks That Save a Small Office 5 Hours a Week"

Hello [Name],

I'm a local educator who designs practical AI assistants for small teams.
I'd like to offer a free 40-minute talk for [chamber/library] members
showing three everyday tasks (emails, customer FAQs, and document
summaries) done safely with free AI tools, including what NOT to put into
AI. No sales pitch during the talk; I'll share a free one-page prompt
guide.

Would a lunch or evening slot in [month] work?

[Name] | [personal email] | [phone]
```

**Script D: direct email to a business owner**
```
Subject: An AI assistant for [their task, e.g., answering quote requests]

Hi [Name],

I help small teams set up AI assistants for one repetitive task, built in
your own account, so you own it. For example, [one-sentence case study].

I'd offer a free 20-minute call to look at one task you'd like off your
plate. If it's a fit, a setup plus a 90-minute team training runs
[price range].

[Name] | [personal email] | [phone]
```

**Free 20-minute "AI task check" call**
1. Ask: "What do you or your staff write or look up over and over?"
2. Ask: "What information must never leave your business?" (customer, health and financial data)
3. Ask: "Which AI accounts do you already use?"
4. Quote one package: training only, build only, or both.

## 4. Rules that apply to both (from the guide)
- Use personal devices, a personal email and personal accounts for all business work.
- Don't use district time or equipment.
- Don't contract with your own district without the conflict-of-interest disclosure. Payroll extra duty is safer.
- Use written quotes and scope. Never sign auto-renewal or indemnity clauses with Indiana schools.
- Track income and set aside 25–35% for taxes.
- Never overstate results; use real survey numbers only.
