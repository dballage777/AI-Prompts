# Deep Dive: Teachers Pay Teachers (TPT) for AI Prompt Products

As of 2026-11-26. I picked TPT because it's where teachers already shop, and its rules changed this fall.

## 1. Seller tiers (TPT's Become a Seller page, opened 2026-11-26, Documented)

| | Basic | Exclusive (new) |
|---|---|---|
| Cost | $29 one-time (waived for existing sellers) | $59.95 per year |
| You keep | 55% of each sale | 77.5% (80% intro rate on sales "until January 1") |
| Transaction fee | $0.30 per resource | $0.15 per resource, on orders under $3 only |
| Condition | None | "Sell eligible digital products exclusively on TPT" |
| Tools | Basic sales reports; 3 Keyword Finder searches a day | Advanced search analytics, discount tools, unlimited Keyword Finder, URL Builder |

- **Premium is retired.** The old Premium tier ended on 2026-10-01 (Reported).
- **The Exclusive fee is yearly, not monthly.** Chalkbeat corrected its story: the fee is "$60 per year."

## 2. Basic or Exclusive? (Inferred)

On a **$10** product:
- **Basic** keeps $5.50 − $0.30 = **$5.20** per sale.
- **Exclusive** keeps **$7.75** per sale.
- The difference is **$2.55 per sale**, so Exclusive pays for itself after about **24 sales a year**.

But Exclusive means you can't sell "eligible digital products" on Payhip, Gumroad or directly to schools. Sellers have started a petition against that rule.

**Recommendation:** start on **Basic**.
- The median TPT store has made only $131 in lifetime sales (2020 study), so most new stores won't reach 24 sales a year at first.
- Basic keeps your other options open, including direct district sales and your own store.
- Review the choice in month 6. If TPT brings in nearly all your sales and you pass about 25 sales a year, Exclusive starts to make sense.
- Confirm what "eligible digital products" covers before upgrading. The term wasn't defined on the page I opened.

## 3. AI policy (effective 2026-11-19, TPT Seller Blog, Documented)

| Not allowed | Allowed (limited AI help) |
|---|---|
| Resources that are "entirely or primarily AI-generated" | AI for brainstorming, research or outlining |
| Covers or thumbnails that are entirely AI-generated | AI copy editing of your own writing |
| AI text with little or no editing | AI decorative elements |
| Titles or descriptions pasted from AI | |
| Unedited AI translations | |

- **Disclosure:** starting in 2027, sellers must disclose some AI assistance when they list or update a resource.
- **Enforcement:** automated tools flag resources and a person reviews every case. Stores with lots of low-effort AI content "will likely be closed without prior notice."
- **TPT's stated goal:** a TPT spokeswoman said seller material "should be unique, expertly crafted, and AI-free" (Chalkbeat, 2026-10-15).
- **Unclear: prompt packs.** The policy doesn't say whether resources *about* AI (prompt packs, Gem guides) are treated differently. A prompt you wrote and tested is your own writing, but TPT hasn't confirmed this. The official FAQ was blocked when I tried to read it.
- **Unclear: existing listings.** It isn't stated whether listings made before the policy will be reviewed. A Change.org petition calls the ban "retroactive," but that is the petition's description, not TPT's.

**What this means for you (Inferred):**
- Your portfolio says the AI drafted much of your work while you directed it. For TPT, the final teacher-facing text needs to be **yours**.
- Use AI only for outlining and copy editing.
- Keep dated drafts as proof of your own work.
- **Ask TPT seller support in writing** before your first listing. A suggested question: "Are teacher-written AI prompt templates and setup guides allowed under the AI policy, and how should AI assistance be disclosed?"

## 4. The market for AI prompt products (sampled 2026-11-26)
- **"ChatGPT prompts for teachers":** the top 20 results cost $5.00–$31.99, mostly $8–$20. Only 1 of the 20 had any reviews.
- **"Gemini Gem for teachers":** 21 results, from free to $20. Setup guides run $10–$15.
- **Inferred:** buyers have few trusted products to choose from. A well-reviewed, clearly teacher-written, tested kit could stand out, but there's no data yet on how much these products sell.

## 5. Earnings: what to expect
- In a 2020 study of 152,393 stores with paid items, the median store had **$131 in lifetime sales**, 34% had sold nothing, and the top 1% took 81% of sales.
- TPT's CEO said in 2022 that the average seller brings in "low single-digit thousands annually."
- IXL's CEO said AI products are "a minuscule fraction of a percent of TPT's sales" (Chalkbeat, 2026-08-03).
- **Plan for (Inferred):** a few to a few dozen sales in the first year from 1–3 products. Growth comes from building a catalog (10+ products), bundles and steady marketing.

## 6. Launch checklist
- [ ] District clearance obtained, or the product rebuilt on your own time and devices.
- [ ] Final text written by you; dated drafts saved.
- [ ] Your own cover image, at least 750×750 px, plus 3 previews.
- [ ] An 80-character title with your search phrase near the start.
- [ ] Best keywords in the first 1–3 sentences of the description.
- [ ] No more than 4 grades tagged.
- [ ] Trademark attribution line included; no "GPT" or "Gemini" in the product name.
- [ ] Only made-up data in samples.
- [ ] A Pinterest business account with vertical pins that link to the listing.
- [ ] A calendar reminder to re-test and review prices every 6 months.
