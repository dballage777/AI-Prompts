Drafted 2026-11-26. For education service centers (ESCs), other districts and conferences.

## One-page session description

**AI for Busy Teachers: Save Time on Catch-Up Lists, Differentiation, and Planning, Safely**

Led by a practicing high school educator who designs and tests AI tools for classroom use. [ADD: your title and years teaching]

**Participants will leave able to:**
1. Turn a gradebook export into a clear "who needs to catch up on what" list in minutes.
2. Make reteach, on-level and enrichment versions of a lesson, and check the AI's answer keys before using them.
3. Write prompts that get reliable results, with a simple four-part pattern.
4. Follow safe-use basics: no student data in AI tools, checking facts, and matching district AI guidance.

**What makes it different:** it's built on tools used in a real high school classroom, with hands-on practice on participants' own (non-student-data) materials. It works with Gemini™, ChatGPT™ or other AI chat tools.
