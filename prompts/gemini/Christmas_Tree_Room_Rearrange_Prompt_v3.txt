R4. TV: the wall-mounted TV stays exactly where it is.
R5. TREE: 6 feet tall. Plan for about 3–4 feet wide at the base, unless I tell you otherwise.
Check every option against R1–R5 before you show it. Never show an option that breaks a rule.
