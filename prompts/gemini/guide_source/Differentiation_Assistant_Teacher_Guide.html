Your Differentiation Assistant

A plain-language guide to the Gemini prompt for reteaching, differentiation, challenge, and co-teaching (version 4)

What it does, in one sentence

You give Gemini your materials (a test, your notes, or a spreadsheet of results), and it walks you step by step to ready-to-use materials that help students who don't get it yet, students who already get it, and everyone in between. Then it delivers them as a Google Doc, Slides, a Form, a Sheet, or text you can copy.

BLUE = Getting started GREEN = Support (not yet) PURPLE = Challenge (got it) GOLD = Whole class ORANGE = Assessment TEAL = Co-teaching PINK = English learners GRAY = Safety & limits

What happens: 7 simple steps

  1. Tell it your goal. Choose from: analyze results, differentiate, reteach, challenge, co-teach, or build assessments.
  2. Share your materials. Upload a PDF, a CSV, a Doc, or a photo, or just describe what you need.
  3. It checks your files and tells you what it found. You confirm the course, grade, and lesson goal.
  4. It shows who needs what. You get a question-by-question results table and groups listed by row number, never by name.
  5. You pick what to make. You only see the options that fit your goal (type ALL to see every option).
  6. You pick the format. Google Doc, Slides, Form, Sheet, or copy-and-paste text.
  7. It shows a short plan, then builds it. It checks every answer, delivers the work in parts if it's long, and offers revisions.

Using your test results (including Pear Assessment)

Do this

Bring your results in

  • Pear Assessment (formerly Edulastic): Gemini can't log in, so download a report table as a CSV (from Insights or Reports). Some reports need a Premium or Enterprise plan.
  • Save the test as a PDF using the print option, then "Save as PDF".
  • Upload both files together. You can delete the names column first.
You get

A clear results table

  • For each question: the skill, % correct, the most common wrong answer, and the likely misconception.
  • Flags: below 60% = reteach, 60–79% = practice, 80%+ = mastered. You can change these.
  • Groups: Mastered / Partial / Not yet, listed by row number so you can match them to names yourself.

How your finished work arrives

You chooseWhat you get
1. Google DocBuilt in Canvas. Click Export to Docs. To print or save a PDF: File → Download → PDF.
2. Google SlidesOne idea per slide, with speaker notes, exported to Slides.
3. Google Form quizQuestions and choices ready to paste into a Form, with correct answers marked.
4. Google SheetClean tables you can export to Sheets.
5. Copy and pasteText in the chat. This is always available.

Every document has the same layout: a Teacher Packet (goal, data summary, how to run it, groups, prep time, answer keys), then Student Pages, each version on its own page, with no answer keys. Long documents come in parts. Type NEXT to get the next part.

Built-in safeguards

Fair for every student
  • Same goal for everyone. Support adds help, not less work. Challenge adds depth, not more work.
  • Groups change based on evidence and have neutral names (no "low" or "gifted" labels).
  • It never sorts students by "learning styles."
Accurate & private
  • Solves every problem and checks every answer key and calculation. Anything uncertain is marked [VERIFY].
  • No student names in its replies, only row numbers.
  • You decide groups, grades, and accommodations. It never reads IEP or 504 documents.

What it can't do

  • It can't log in to Pear Assessment, Google Classroom, or any other website. Download your files and upload them.
  • It can't send, post, or save files for you. It drafts them, and you export or copy them.
  • Export buttons depend on your school account. If they're missing, use copy-and-paste text.
  • It can make mistakes. Always review before using it with students.
